# The Undersong — illustrated work in progress

An editable Arke Studio world containing The Ledger of Nights, its generated artwork and
the production work completed on 19 October 2026.

- 16 novel chapters, 42,497 words and 244 attached illustrations.
- World Bible, character looks, location views and six bound prop references.
- Retained generated artwork, selected production history and an EPUB.
- Six illustrated single-narrator audiobook chapter players, approximately 123 minutes.
- Video pilot plan: 15 shots / 117 planned seconds, with four stills.

**Audiobook unfinished:** only six of sixteen chapters have completed exports. Remaining
narration and partial production work are included where available, not represented as finished.

**Video unfinished:** no completed H3 clips or final video episode. Supporting cast references,
a North Salt location sheet, and final editorial / illustration review are also pending.

Download the ZIP, extract it into a new folder and open that folder as a world in Arke Studio.
Arke Studio v0.5.61 also offers this download in **Settings → Sample world**, checks its integrity
and installs it under a fresh identity. Existing worlds are not updated or overwritten.
The lightweight automated-test fixture is separate and is not included in this delivery.
