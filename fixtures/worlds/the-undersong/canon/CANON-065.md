If the gods were ordered out, and echoes are acts of disobedience, then the question nobody in
this record has yet said aloud is: what happens if the Authority Before Names notices?

Perhaps nothing. Perhaps it does not care, and two fragments arguing over one small harbour are
beneath whatever counts as its attention. Perhaps the Departure was complete the moment it was
obeyed, and what was left behind is litter, not defiance.

Or perhaps the Departure was never an event. Perhaps it is a standing rule — in force this
whole time, the way a law is in force on an empty road — and somewhere in the past year, as one
echo weakens and the other approaches remembering, something has begun to break it.

> Room note: this stays distant, and the Authority stays absent. No face, no motive, no
> dialogue, no cult that speaks for it — the moment it has any of those it becomes a character,
> and characters are smaller than absences. It is permitted exactly three words in the whole of
> this world, and they are already carved somewhere old: AND THEY WENT.
