The harbour is not the only place where something was left behind. It is only the place this
record knows.

A valley survives every wildfire; its echo was told PRESERVE WHAT GROWS HERE, and no longer
distinguishes people from invasive plants. On an ancient battlefield, travellers hear thousands
marching underground; that echo was told HOLD THE BORDER, and the country whose border it holds
has been gone twelve centuries. A distant city has recorded no deliberate killing in
eighty-three years and calls this a miracle of civilisation, unaware that something is removing
violent intent from its people — and unaware of what else it may be taking. Hundreds of miles
from any coast stands a working lighthouse; every forty-seven years it lights a road that does
not otherwise exist. Children lost in a certain forest always come home. Always. Even the ones
who were lost decades ago. Some come home unchanged.

A few people have noticed that these things rhyme. Not an order or a church — something
smaller, older, and scattered, known to each other as **Surveyors**. They collect impossible
events, repeated symbols, architectural anomalies, traditions with no memory of their purpose.
Their working theory is that civilisation is littered with ancient instructions still
executing, and their whole method fits on a signet ring: *find the command.* Because once you
know what an echo was told to do, nearly everything about it becomes legible.

The difficulty — the thing that should keep any Surveyor honest and does not always — is that
the language the commands were given in may not map onto human concepts at all (CANON-052).

> Room note: no Surveyor appears in either current production. This entry exists so the world
> is larger than the harbour, not so someone can arrive in act three with answers.

**Amended at revision 104.** One more, from further out than the rest: somewhere in a future
this record will never reach, an expedition finds a dead civilisation's defensive fleet still
on station — **the Sleeping Fleet** — faithfully protecting "citizens" defined by a biological
standard nobody alive can meet. Filed here because it is the whole cosmology in one image: a
promise, outliving everyone it was made to, still keeping (CANON-071).
