The Departure (CANON-053) was not understood by the Authors as a permanent abandonment. At
least some of them expected to come back — to witness what humanity became after being allowed
to grow on its own account — and the leaving makes better engineering sense read that way: some
surviving echo architectures assume later maintenance, clarification, relief or handover, not
eternal unattended operation (CANON-068).

Humanity holds one especially consequential surviving testimony. In this cosmology, Jesus was
one of the messengers associated with the celestial order, and his promise of an eventual
return is genuine evidence that the absence was expected to end. What the record can say stops
there, deliberately: later interpretation supplies no technical account of the event, no
surviving evidence establishes him as an echo, and his exact ontology, authority and
relationship to the Authors are unknown. The promise survives. The timetable does not.

> They left us the world. They did not say they would never see it again.

The return has not occurred — or has not occurred in any form humanity or the surviving echoes
can recognise. No echo can contact its Author, obtain a status response, or explain the delay.
Scholarship calls the interval **the Long Silence**: not proof the Authors are dead, not proof
the promise failed — the enormous unexplained gap between a promised return and a world still
waiting.

Speculation inside the world runs where it will: the Authority extended the prohibition; the
Authors met catastrophe elsewhere; time passes differently for them; humanity has not reached
the expected threshold; the mechanism failed; the promise refers to something already
misunderstood. No human or echo source confirms any of it. An echo may remember that contact
was expected; it cannot see upward into its Author's present circumstances.

The consequence is the one that matters: the promise has never functioned as a rescue. The
town, and the species, have had to act without knowing whether their makers return tomorrow,
in ten thousand years, or never. The silence intensifies responsibility. It does not suspend it.
