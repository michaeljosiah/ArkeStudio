The draw falls on whoever is nearest the water when a tide is called (CANON-046). The warning
roll tells subscribers to be elsewhere (CANON-049).

The Half-Hitches have been on the warning roll, in good standing, for six generations. They are
riggers and quiet-cargo carriers. Their living is the north gate and the quay, and the quay is
the last place in the harbour a warning can move you out of, because moving out of it is the one
thing that costs you the day.

Nine of the thirty-one residents of the Quiet Ward are Half-Hitches. They paid every tithe. They
received every notice. They read them.

**Open, because the Council has never framed it as a question.** Its actuary has twice been asked
why one name recurs and has twice reported that the distribution is within tolerance — which is
arithmetically true, and is not an answer to what was asked. There is no finding of fault
available, because no fault was committed: a subscription service delivered its product, and its
customers were unable to use it, and there is no box on any form for that.

Bray owes a debt he has stopped counting. It is arrears on the warning roll. He has been
receiving notices on credit for years and standing on the quay through every one of them.

**Amended at revision 73.** The actuary is named now: Perrin Tallow, in the post eleven years,
well regarded, meticulous. Naming this changes nothing about why the pattern exists — CANON-046
and the ordinary economics of who cannot afford to leave the quay (CANON-049) still fully
account for it, and this entry is not a mystery about Perrin. It is a mystery about six
generations of one family and a Council that never had to lie.

What Perrin actually is (CANON-054) is a different, adjacent horror, not the solution to this
one. Do not let a future draft collapse them into a single villain. The Half-Hitches were not
targeted. They were poor, and reachable, and that was always enough on its own.

> Do not settle this thread by exposing a villain. It settles, if it ever does, by somebody
> finding the form and discovering there is no box on it.
