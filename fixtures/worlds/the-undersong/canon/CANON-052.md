Two hundred and six years ago the god did not drown, and — this record must correct itself here
— it did not cut a piece from its own body either, whatever that read as at the time. What is
under this harbour is not the god. The god left, with the rest of them, at the Withdrawal
(CANON-053). What is under this harbour is what the god left behind on its way out: an echo,
made for exactly one purpose, and placed here because this is where the other one was.

This is why the Weigh-House has never found a body (CANON-031). Nothing died, and nothing that
was ever alive is down there to have died. An echo does not decompose. It works.

Its work is holding the Unremembered (CANON-054) — the other echo, left by another god that did
not share this one's opinion of the town — bound, quiet, and forgetting. Every verse is a
renewal of that binding. Every tide-calling is a hand lending itself to the same working, which
is why the cost is drawn from hearing: the binding is a working of sound — celestial instruction,
heard by the few who can hear it as song — and the toll of joining a working of sound has always
been paid in the currency it is made of.

The record has called this echo "the god" for two hundred years because the town needed a word
and that was the nearest one. It is not a god. It is closer to a promise a god made on its way
out the door, kept faithfully, alone, for longer than anyone who asked for the promise has been
alive to thank it for.

**Amended at revision 68.** Previously recorded here as the god's own body, self-severed, holding
its own wound shut, with the Chorister as the part that was cut away. That was wrong in every
particular except the shape of the wrongness: something was cut away and something was kept, but
neither was the god, and the Chorister was never the wound. See CANON-054 for what actually was,
and characters/the-chorister.md for what the Chorister actually is.

**Amended at revision 77.** The few who know part of the truth have a name for it, and the
record adopts it: **the Keeper Below**. It may never have had one of its own. It has no flesh —
no skeleton waits under the seabed, no leviathan can be woken — and exists through pressure,
resonance, current and pattern. The harbour has effectively become its body: water moving
through submerged streets, stone groaning under tides, chains striking quays, fog horns
answering one another. These are not merely sounds. They are the components of a language.

Its whole command, so far as any command survives, is two words:

> KEEP THEM.

Not *save humanity*. Not *defeat evil*. Keep **these people** — or keep **this place**; no
surviving evidence settles the difference, and the difference has never once mattered less than
it does now. An echo's command language does not map cleanly onto human meaning (CANON-055).
KEEP THEM could mean protect them, imprison them, preserve their line, keep them from leaving,
keep them alive, keep them human, or keep them hidden. Two hundred years of its behaviour
support the kindest reading. They also support the others (CANON-066).
