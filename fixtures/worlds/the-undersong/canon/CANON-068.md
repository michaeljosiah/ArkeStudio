An echo is not a god and not merely a machine. It is an **authored intelligence**: a persistent
fragment of a departed celestial mind, instantiated so that part of its intention can continue
acting after its Author has gone. To an ancient witness it is divine residue. To a far-future
scientist it is a persistent autonomous agent whose full cognitive structure occupies degrees
of freedom ordinary physics cannot reach. Neither description invalidates the other — a Bronze
Age voice says *she is the goddess of the sea*, a later one says *she is a post-biological
intelligence capable of authoring an ocean-scale agent*, and both may predict the same reality.

An echo has three layers, and humans only ever meet the outermost:

**The Core** — the intelligence itself, persisting in dimensions ordinary observation cannot
enter. Not reachable by digging deeper. A three-dimensional object passing through a
two-dimensional sheet shows the sheet only its changing cross-section; humanity encounters
cross-sections.

**The Manifestation** — the local body its Author permitted: a harbour, a succession of human
hosts, a forest, a fleet, a city's infrastructure. Embodiment is authored, not species-wide,
which is why no two echoes need resemble each other.

**The Interface** — how it touches local minds: song, dreams, symbols, field effects, biology.

An Author does more than set a goal. The design specifies a **Mandate** (what must be
accomplished), an **Embodiment Law** (what may become its body), a Domain, an Operational
Authority, a Resource Model, an Interface — and, if the Author is wise, a Termination
Condition, a Failsafe State, and a Succession. The gods were very good at specifying what their
agents should do. They may have been much worse at specifying when they should stop.

The record notes, without vanity, that the strongest modern analogy will eventually be
humanity's own: model, objective, execution environment, permissions, runtime budget,
interface, persistence beyond the maker. The gods did not leave demons behind. They left
instructions. And the day humanity begins delegating intention into persistent systems of its
own, somebody will recognise the pattern and say the three words this cosmology has been
waiting its whole length for:

> So are we.

What people call magic, on this reading, is ancient infrastructure met without a manual: an
incantation is a command interface, a relic is a key, a bloodline gift is an embodiment law
that checks lineage, a haunting is a damaged interface, a miracle is old machinery honouring a
permission that never expired. The explanation deepens the mythology. It does not debunk it.
