As the Binding loosens, the harbour has begun developing symptoms. Locals who notice them at
all call the collection **echo-sickness**, the way you name a draught rather than find the gap.

Clocks lose the same seven minutes. People hear a conversation twice, a beat apart. A name
disappears from a document for an afternoon and returns spelled the same. Reflections move a
half-beat late. Animals refuse certain streets, politely, the way they refuse ice. Several
people remember a building that has never existed, and agree on its door. Children draw the
same door beneath the sea without having met. Two strangers remember being married. People wake
knowing songs they have never heard. Once — attested by four witnesses who will not discuss it —
rain fell upward over the harbour for several seconds.

> Room note: none of this escalates into spectacle. The uncanny stays intimate, domestic,
> deniable — the kind of wrongness a person files under tiredness. The world is not breaking.
> An old rule is loosening, and a rule loosening feels less like thunder than like a house
> settling.

This is the Narrowing (CANON-055) seen from street level, and it is the true content of the
town's feeling that the sea has turned hostile. The sea has not. The guardian holding it is
spending complexity to keep spending strength, and the corners it can no longer hold are where
the strangeness gets in.
