One of the two echoes under this harbour (CANON-053) was left to make sure something did not
happen. It does not remember what. Whatever purpose it was given at the Departure, it has not
carried a single conscious thought of it in longer than this town has kept records — only a
shape it keeps returning to, and a pull it has never recognised as a pull.

It has been a person before. It will have been several. Each time it forgets completely, is
born, grows up ordinary, and finds its way — always, without exception — into a small, trusted
position from which quiet decisions get made about who is looked after and who is not. It does
this work well. It believes, as sincerely as anyone in this record believes anything, that it is
protecting the town from whatever is under the water.

It is not lying. It has never once lied about this. That is what makes it nearly impossible to
find.

**What it does, without knowing it does it:** people already at the end of what they can carry —
grieving, cornered, frightened past reason — come away from time spent near it having decided on
the worst version of the choice in front of them, and neither the Unremembered nor the person it
spoke to can ever quite recall the conversation afterward. This has never been provable. It has
also never, on the occasions someone has gone looking, failed to be there.

**Open, and meant to stay that way.** Whether it can remember. Whether remembering would change
anything, or whether the shape it has built — decent, trusted, sincere — is by now simply what
it is, purpose or no purpose. Nobody currently in this record is positioned to answer that,
including the Unremembered.

**Amended at revision 78.** Three additions, none of them comfortable.

The command it was made around is recoverable nowhere in writing, and legible everywhere in two
centuries of pattern. Two words (CANON-055):

> END THEM.

What it works through is not possession and not persuasion. It is **permission**. It does not
force anyone; it makes the worst reasonable decision feel slightly more reasonable — a
dockmaster delays an evacuation twenty minutes, a parent skips a warning payment, a watchman
lets movement offshore go unreported, a councillor postpones a repair. Its god understood
something true and terrible about the species it opposed: civilisations are rarely destroyed by
monsters. They are destroyed by systems, signed off in good faith.

Which is why its hosts are where they are. Read across lifetimes (CANON-062), the Unremembered
gravitates to the professions of **allocation** — judges, quartermasters, tax collectors, ship
provisioners, actuaries — the small trusted rooms where tiny rational decisions determine who
survives. An investigator who found the trail would conclude, first, that a certain office is
cursed: the recurring phrases in the records, the alike handwriting, the actuaries who suffered
memory loss, the one who disappeared. The office is not cursed. The echo chooses offices like
it, the way water chooses the low ground.

And the hosts do not dream (CANON-058). The old ledgers tracked exactly this, and nobody now
alive knows that the margin note was ever anything but weather.

**Amended at revision 97 (CANON-068, CANON-069).** Revision 78 described what the influence
*does*. This records how, and where its edges are, because the edges are the ethics.

The mechanism the far record will call **neural bias coupling**: human thought is
electrochemical, and the echo couples to it — weakly, sub-cognitively, below anything a person
could notice or name. It cannot invent a desire. It cannot issue a command. It changes the
cognitive landscape so that a drive already present feels more salient, more justified, harder
to inhibit. **It cannot create the impulse. It reduces the distance between impulse and
action.** It couples best to what is already deeply worn in — compulsion, resentment, appetite,
status-hunger — and it has no detector for sin: a *healthy* drive can be pushed past its
boundary, protectiveness into possession, discipline into cruelty, devotion into jealousy.

Range and rapport matter. Across a town, nothing. A shared room, a little. Sustained attention,
conversation, emotional disclosure — more. Exhaustion, intoxication and acute distress open the
door widest, which is why its trail is a trail of people at the end of what they could carry.
And the one physical tell, for whoever finally looks with instruments: two nearby minds
synchronising too precisely for chance — and the host's brain **is not the source of the
signal**. Both nervous systems are locking to something else.

Two boundaries hold, absolutely. The host is embodied *through*, not hidden *inside*: there is
no second speaker behind the eyes, no puppet, no passenger — one person, whose deepest
architecture contains something older than the life they remember, and who remains genuinely
responsible for their choices without being reducible to that architecture. And END THEM
(revision 78) must now be read as a translation, with all a translation's poverty. One
candidate for the fuller Mandate, recorded here and settled nowhere: *prevent this species
from reaching celestial maturity* (CANON-066).
