An echo does not pay primarily for thought. It pays for **being here**. Holding a coherent
intersection between a higher-dimensional Core and ordinary spacetime costs continuously
(presence), costs more when acting (action), and fails in a particular way when underfunded:
not death but bad projection — memory bleed, distorted embodiment, mistranslated Mandate
(coherence). Power is not only what an echo can do. Power is also what it costs to remain
present long enough to do it.

The durable ones are the efficient ones. An elegant Author does not force reality to behave
unnaturally when existing matter, biology or planetary energy can carry the load — and the more
an echo rides an existing substrate, the cheaper its presence, and the more its behaviour is
constrained by what it rides. This is why apparently mythic habits are functional: a thing
sleeps for centuries because wakefulness is expensive; a thing keeps to its reef, its fault
line, its bloodline, because the substrate pays its rent.

**The Keeper's economy.** It does not make the harbour's tides; it rides them. The harbour is
body, interface and power source at once, and the Seven Anchors (CANON-057) keep the coupling
cheap. As anchors and inherited infrastructure degrade, more of its budget goes to merely
remaining present — so communication simplifies and intervention grows rare, not necessarily
because the Core is failing but because **Earth has become expensive**. It may also be running
far past its Operational Horizon: built for maintenance, relief or a returning Author
(CANON-067) that never came, holding a failsafe paradox no design anticipated — below safe
tolerance it should shut down, and shutting down releases the thing it contains. It has likely
spent generations in permanent degraded failsafe, shedding everything optional to keep three
functions alive: remain manifested, maintain the Binding, keep them.

**The Unremembered's advantage.** The opposite design. Human biology already gathers energy,
repairs tissue, senses, moves and speaks; its Core needs only enough coupling to bias what is
already running. Presence is extraordinarily cheap. Control is correspondingly thin. Physical
grandeur measures nothing — the exhausted environmental giant may hold an intact, enormous
mind, and the modest host-bound rider renews its body every lifetime and lets biology pay.

**The survivorship effect.** Humanity does not meet a fair sample of celestial engineering.
The systems that shut down correctly are gone. What survives into the present is precisely the
unfinished: mandates whose conditions never triggered, failsafes at war with their own
purpose, bodies too efficient to die, successors that never arrived. Ancient failure does not
imply primitive engineering. Most of what remains is brilliant work running beyond every
assumption under which safe operation was defined.
