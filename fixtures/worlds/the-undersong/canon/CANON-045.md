title: The ledger is never wrong
status: settled
introducedAt: 43
settledAt: 44
links: [CANON-019, CANON-002, the-vigil]
---

Every tide the Vigil's ledger records as observed is the tide the harbour had. This has been
audited twice and held both times.

It is a rule about the record, not about the water. It does not say the ledger is honest; it
says the ledger is accurate, and those come apart in exactly one case — when the entry is
written before the tide, by somebody who is going to make it true.
