# Saltlight — treatment

A short film. One night's watch on the Vigil, told at the pace of the water.

Maren takes the dusk watch alone. The tide tables say neap; the harbour says otherwise. Bells,
one night early. She calls the tide back a hand-span at a time and pays for it in the usual
coin, and by slack water she knows two things: the verse has risen early, and somebody on the
rail beside her was never taught to whistle — because they never needed the warning.

Third act is the Chorister's, and we do not subtitle the song.
