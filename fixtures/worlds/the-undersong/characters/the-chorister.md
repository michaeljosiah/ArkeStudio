## Essence
Nobody has seen the Chorister arrive or leave. They are simply at the rail some nights,
humming the harbour into order. What they keep, and for whom, is not settled.

## Appearance
Unsettled. Grey robes that read as wet in any light. A face people describe differently and
each swear to — old woman, young man, child, sailor, drowned girl. Witnesses agree on exactly
one detail, and it is not visual: they know, without being able to say how, that **it is
singing**. Even when they cannot hear it. It has appeared at the end of empty piers, inside
fog, on the surface of the water, and — once, per the Keeper of Works' sealed instructions —
never yet indoors. The instruction for that case is one word long: evacuate (CANON-056).

## Voice
Answers the question you did not ask. Has, within the last year, begun saying "the last verse" —
never as a guess, always as something already true. Nobody remembers hearing the phrase from
them before this year (CANON-044, CANON-051).

## Truth
