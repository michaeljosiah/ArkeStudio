## Essence
Nobody has seen the Chorister arrive or leave. They are simply at the rail some nights,
humming the harbour into order. What they keep, and for whom, is not settled.

## Appearance
Unsettled. Grey robes that read as wet in any light. A face people describe differently and
each swear to.
