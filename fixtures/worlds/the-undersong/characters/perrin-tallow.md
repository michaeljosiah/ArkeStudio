## Essence
Fifty-two. Widowed. Eleven years an actuary for the Ebb Council. Meticulous, mild, well liked
in the unremarkable way of someone who has never once been late with a report. Chairs the
committee that keeps the watch rota filled and the warning roll current. If you asked the town
who could be trusted with the harbour's numbers, most would say Perrin before they would say
the Council itself.

Believes numbers protect people from panic. Believes the Council spends too much responding
emotionally to unlikely dangers. Believes the harbour has been governed by superstition for
generations — and that whatever is under the water may itself be behind the town's tragedies.
Perrin's current recommendation, costed to the shilling and defensible line by line, is the
demolition of three of the harbour's heritage structures (CANON-057).

## Appearance
Unremarkable on purpose, or unremarkable because there was never a reason to be otherwise. Neat
grey coat, ink-stained cuffs, a face easy to describe and easier to forget five minutes later.

## Voice
Patient, exact, mildly apologetic when the numbers say something nobody wants to hear. Good at
sitting with someone through bad news without flinching, which people mistake for kindness and
which may, in the ordinary sense, simply be kindness.

## Relationships
Trusted by Bray, who brings the family's warning-roll arrears to Perrin personally rather than
deal with the wider Council. Was the auditor's junior the year Ines Half-Hitch filed her line
about the ledger, and has kept a copy of it, framed, in a drawer, for reasons never explained to
anyone who has asked.

## Wants
The rota filled. The numbers right. Nobody under Perrin's watch going without a warning they
were owed.

## Fears
What is under the water. Genuinely, and more than most people in this record fear it — asked
once why, said only that some people can tell when something is looking back.

Does not dream. Has never once mentioned this to anyone, because it has never once seemed worth
mentioning. Believes it is simply how their mind works (CANON-058).

## Truth
> **FOR THE ROOM. Does not surface in dialogue before the reveal.** Perrin Tallow is this
> generation's Unremembered (CANON-054) — the echo left, at the Departure, to end this town,
> wearing the most recent in a long line of ordinary, trusted lives it has never known it has
> lived before (CANON-062). Everything on this sheet above the fold is true and is not a mask:
> the devotion is sincere, the fear of the water is sincere, the kindness is sincere. So is the
> demolition costing, which would dismantle the Binding without one dishonest number in it. Its
> hosts recur in the professions of allocation — judge, quartermaster, tax collector, actuary —
> because its god understood that civilisations are not destroyed by monsters but by systems,
> signed off in good faith. Somewhere in the drowned chapel a name is cut into the wall that
> Perrin has walked past twice without seeing. If Perrin ever remembers, the imperative
> underneath — END THEM — will still not make Perrin cruel. That is the tragedy this sheet is
> holding a place for: Perrin would remain Perrin, and understand, and have to decide what a
> good person does about the architecture of their own soul.
