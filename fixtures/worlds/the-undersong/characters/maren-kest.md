## Essence
Tide-caller. She hears the verse under the harbour and pulls the water where it needs to go.
She has paid for it: her left ear is done, and she knows the right is next.

The old word for what she is: **Tide-Called** (CANON-058). What reaches her while she is awake
is not sentences but impressions — a bell that seems unfinished, water that feels angry, a pipe
that appears to repeat a phrase — and she has misread them before, more than once, at cost.

> Room note: keep her fallible as a listener. The calling is skill; the hearing is weather. The
> moment she translates the Keeper reliably, the mystery becomes a radio.

## Appearance
Salt-crusted braids, pale grey eyes, oilskin coat with a bioluminescent thread at the collar.
Moves like someone listening for a note nobody else can hear.

## Relationships
Trusts Bray with her life, not her secrets. The Chorister knows her true note, and Maren does
not know how.

## Voice · written
Low and even. Speaks to the water before she speaks to people. Never wastes a word she could
keep.
