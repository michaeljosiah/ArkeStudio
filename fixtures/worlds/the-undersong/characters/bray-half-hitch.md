## Essence
A rigger who can tie a knot for every weather and a story for every knot. Runs cargo the Ebb
Council pretends not to tax. Loyal past sense.

## Appearance
Wind-burned, broad, forearms mapped with rope scars. Wears three belts and needs one.

## Relationships
Would sink for Maren. Owes the Ebb Council a debt he has stopped counting, which is not the
same as it being paid.

## Voice · written
Fast, warm, always halfway into a story. Goes very quiet when the water goes wrong.
