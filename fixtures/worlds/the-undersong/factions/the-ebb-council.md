## Essence
The harbour's shadow government: nine chairs, seven filled, two kept empty for the drowned.
They tax what moves, pardon what pays, and keep the old agreements with the water.

## Wants
The Undersong kept quiet. The tithe flowing. Maren Kest on a leash she cannot feel.

## Fears
The god waking hungry. A tide-caller who stops asking permission. Empty chairs filling.

## Offices
The Council is not a cult and must never play as one. Most councillors know nothing. A handful
know fragments. One office knows more: the **Keeper of Works**, supervisor of harbour
infrastructure, whose every holder receives a sealed document from their predecessor —
seventeen instructions, no explanations (CANON-056). The instructions are followed to the
letter by people who would be embarrassed to say why, which is precisely how they have
survived. The Council's currently sitting mistake is procedural, not malicious: it is letting
an actuary cost the heritage structures (CANON-057), because no rule was ever written saying it
must not, because nobody who understood the reason imagined the question would be asked.
