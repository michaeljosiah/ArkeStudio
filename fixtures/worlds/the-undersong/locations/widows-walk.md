## Look
A stone pier extending into dangerous water and arriving nowhere. No mooring rings were ever
fitted; no boat has ever tied up to it; no record explains why it was built. It is simply very
old, very solid, and very much in the way.

## Sound
Wrong. The water under it never quite agrees with the wind over it, and people who fish from
its end do not go back.

## Customs
Nobody walks it after dark, on no particular authority. The Council's demolition shortlist has
it first of three — Perrin Tallow's costing is thorough, correct, and entirely reasonable.

At extreme low tide, a second pier is visible directly beneath it. Perfectly aligned. Built
first.
