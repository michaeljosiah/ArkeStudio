## Look
A long shed of tarred board at the landward end of the Saltmarket, floor worn into channels by
two centuries of barrows. Brass scales the size of a door. Everything in it is calibrated and
nothing in it is level.

## Sound
Chain, counterweight, the particular argument of people being weighed accurately.

## Customs
The weigh-house keeps its own tide record, independently of the Vigil, and has since 1747. That
this record has never once disagreed with the ledger is the reason CANON-045 survived its
audits — and is the second place to look if anyone ever wants to break it.
