## Look
Stalls under sailcloth awnings, everything rimed white. Fish, rope, lamp-oil, rumours. The
gutters run brine at high tide and the sellers stand on crates without comment.

## Sound
Haggling in three port dialects. Gulls. A hurdy-gurdy that only knows sad tunes.

## Customs
Prices double after dark. The Ebb Council's tithe-box sits at the north gate, and everyone
pays it something, even if it is only a nod.
