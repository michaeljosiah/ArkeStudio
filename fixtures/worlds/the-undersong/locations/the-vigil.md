## Look
A watchtower of black stone at the harbour mouth, ringed by a rail worn smooth by two hundred
years of gripping hands. Lamps burn green-white. The waterline never quite agrees with the tide
tables.

## Sound
Bells under the water on the worst nights. The rail hums a fifth below the wind.

## Customs
Nobody whistles on the Vigil. Watch changes at slack water, never on the hour.
