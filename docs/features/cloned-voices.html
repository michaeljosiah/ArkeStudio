Cloned voices

A recording, with a reader you choose.

Record speech or import an audio file into the world's voice library. A connected hosted reader can use that recording to speak a character's lines.

Kokoro supplies local preset voices. Cloned recordings are read through Mistral, BreezeBlue or Fish Audio. ComfyUI is used for images and video.

What you need

  • A supported hosted reader connected in Settings → Providers.
  • A WAV or MP3 recording of one person speaking, at least three seconds long.
  • The speaker's agreement to clone and use their voice.

The recording is saved in the world. Reading it through a hosted provider sends it to that provider; Arke asks for that voice and vendor before the first upload.

Connect a reader

Open Settings → Providers and connect Mistral, BreezeBlue or Fish Audio. Their available voices appear in the character's voice chooser, with availability and price.

Make and assign a voice

  1. Open the character's Voice page

    Choose Upload a recording, then record with your microphone or select a WAV or MP3 file.

  2. Name and describe the voice

    State its language and confirm the speaker agreed. The description helps match the voice to a character.

  3. Save the recording

    The voice chooser opens on your saved voices. Saving does not select a paid reader or change the character's assignment.

  4. Choose a reader

    Select a connected reader for the saved voice, review its price, then preview or assign it. If none is connected, connect one in Settings → Providers.

The rules

One recording can be used by several characters and supported readers. Provider upload confirmation and paid-read approval are separate. Removing a reader does not delete the world's recordings, assignments or accepted takes.

Behind the scenes

The library stores the recording and its identity with the world. Hosted reads follow the normal queue, pricing, verification and caching path. Mistral receives the reference clip with a read; BreezeBlue and Fish Audio use a saved provider voice.
