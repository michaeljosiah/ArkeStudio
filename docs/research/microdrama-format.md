# The vertical microdrama format

**Gathered:** 2026-10-08, by web research. **Used by:** the adaptation method
([adaptation-method.md](adaptation-method.md)) and Arke's microdrama defaults.

The vertical short drama as ReelShort, DramaBox, ShortMax and GoodShort make it, and the Chinese
短剧 it grew from, with a focus on how novels and web novels are adapted into it. For African
audiences, see [african-short-drama.md](african-short-drama.md): the norms below are the US and
Chinese ones and do not transfer as they stand.

Several Chinese-language sources (Xinhua, woshipm, China Writer, Yicai, Jiemian) could be read only
as search excerpts and are marked *(excerpt)*. Variety, The Hollywood Reporter and The Ankler were
paywalled. The best full sources were the Producers Guild's *Produced By* (June/July 2026), Sixth
Tone, the Television Academy's Emmy magazine, Stage 32 and Scriptnotes. Sources marked *(vendor)*
are tool companies writing about the format: rules of thumb, not practice.

## Structure

**The series.** Instead of three acts, repeating conflict-and-resolution cycles, each episode ending
on a cliffhanger ([Sogang thesis, 2025](https://dcollection.sogang.ac.kr/dcollection/srch/srchDetail/000000079419)).
The common romance-and-revenge engine runs: humiliation; a hidden identity or power; small public
payoffs; bigger antagonists; a misunderstanding that separates; the reveal; a final public payoff.
Escalate about every ten episodes to stop drop-off, and plan from the paywall episode outwards
([Slakter, Stage 32](https://www.stage32.com/lounge/screenwriting/What-15-Years-of-TV-Writing-Taught-Me-About-Vertical-Drama-And-What-It-Didnt)).
Chinese practice treats the first three episodes as decisive *(excerpt)*.

**The paywall episode.** Lock at a drop that comes right after a small payoff *(excerpt)*; one
vendor guide puts the big reveals one or two episodes past the threshold, since a question makes
people pay and an answer satisfies them *(vendor)*.

**The episode** (timings vary between templates):

1. **0–5 s, cold open:** mid-conflict, a face, a shock line. Sources cite a 3-second rule
   ([LA Film School](https://www.lafilm.edu/?p=31325)) and a 5-second rule
   ([*Produced By*](https://producersguild.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Verticals-Microdrama.pdf));
   China's "3-7-21": a hook by 3 s, a small peak by 7, a result by 21 *(excerpt)*.
2. **About 5–15 s:** who wants what, and what is in the way.
3. **The middle:** pressure that can be filmed, and exactly one turn. An emotional beat every
   20–30 seconds ([*Produced By*](https://producersguild.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Verticals-Microdrama.pdf)).
4. **The last 5–10 s, the button:** a revelation, reversal, deadline or intrusion
   ([InkTip](https://www.inktip.com/article_single.php?a_id=259)). Cut on the question, not the answer.

## Adapting a novel into it

**Who does it.** Studios hire writers to adapt web novels; a Chinese report found most microdrama
writers it spoke to were former web novelists *(excerpt)*; several US apps grew out of reading apps
([*Produced By*](https://producersguild.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Verticals-Microdrama.pdf)).
Chinese producers now favour short stories of 10k–80k characters, adapted to 40–80 episodes
*(excerpt)*, and one write-up says a long web novel's real value may be 30–40 emotional hooks
*(excerpt)*.

**Mapping.** At 60–100 episodes, a 100k-word novel is about 1,000–1,700 words an episode. Vendor
rules of thumb say a chapter an episode, splitting dense chapters and merging thin ones
([invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/novel-to-ai-micro-drama/), *vendor*). Practice does not map
evenly: pull the peaks out first, give each episode one, and over-weight the first ten.

## Character and dialogue

Clarity over subtlety. DramaBox's LA studio head says the format both shows and tells, because every
second must carry information or feeling
([*Produced By*](https://producersguild.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Verticals-Microdrama.pdf)).
Dialogue is on the nose, the title is the logline, faces and hands fill the frame, the cast in a
scene is small. A writer was told her dialogue was too subtle and her hooks lost viewers within
seconds ([Sixth Tone](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1017576)).

## What audiences reward and punish

- **Retention:** about 27% on day one, under 10% by day seven, about 2% by day thirty; the drop
  follows the end of the free episodes ([Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/the-microdrama-volume-vs-value-paradox/)).
- **Rewarded:** instant emotional payoff, visible justice, the underdog's rise, a satisfying ending.
- **Punished:** anything that drags, confuses or delays the hook
  ([*Produced By*](https://producersguild.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Verticals-Microdrama.pdf));
  padding, and "satisfaction fatigue" *(excerpt)*.
- **Dark and supernatural** themes do well in North America — werewolves, vampires, vengeance
  ([Sixth Tone](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1014527)). Writers report pressure toward humiliation
  and power-imbalance tropes ([Sixth Tone](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1017576)); an adaptation
  should let the author set those limits.

## Slow-burn literary romance in the format

Synthesis of the above, not a sourced technique:

1. Make the conflict visible: confrontations, choices and costs the camera can see.
2. Put the leads in each other's way in episode one, so the slow part is resistance, not meeting.
3. Turn the slow burn into a ladder of milestones, the confession near the middle, every episode
   still turning.
4. Let one line of voiceover carry inner life at peaks, then show the behaviour.
5. Keep the scenes readers love; merge heavily elsewhere.
6. Make moral greyness readable at a glance, or it confuses ([Sixth Tone](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1017576)).

## Evidence notes

Episode length, free-episode count and budgets vary by source, year and platform. Second-by-second
templates come from vendors and disagree. The short-story trend, the 30–40 hooks figure and Chinese
pay and pace figures rest on search excerpts. More than 90% of China's 2026 microdramas are reported
to be AI-generated ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/microdrama/)).
