# 1 · Requirements

## 1.1 Summary

Arke measures a great deal about the machine it runs on and states all of it honestly. The
engine's readiness walk names the first thing that failed rather than reporting a generic
unavailable. Setup names the component, its size, its state and its reason. The environment
check carries free disk, the path budget and the native index. Probes carry graphics memory.
The queue carries its lanes. `ClientState.app` holds around thirty such facts, all of them
current, all of them true.

Not one of them is joined to another.

Settings · Local runtime is six groups on a rail — This machine, Components, Voice, ComfyUI,
Local models, Authoring harness — and each speaks only for itself. When Local · Draft Video is
disabled because its weights are missing, and the weights are blocked because the mapped drive
is full, the product states both facts in two different groups, in two different vocabularies,
and leaves the reader to notice that the second explains the first. **The correlator is the
user.** That is the gap this specification closes.

It closes it with derivation, not with a model. Everything needed to make that join is already
measured and already deterministic; putting an inference engine in the middle would make a
reproducible answer slower, less reliable and impossible to test. What is missing is a small
amount of arithmetic and one honest vocabulary.

The unit is a **finding**: a condition, the facts that establish it, the cause in one measured
clause, and the control that resolves it — where that control is one the product already has.
The snapshot is the set of findings true right now, derived on demand from state the app is
already holding, carrying no store of its own.

Two surfaces consume it: a view that shows the findings in severity order beside their
controls, and a support export safe to paste into an issue.

This is deliberately the substrate and not the advisor. A system agent that answers open
questions in prose is a separate specification, and this snapshot is the tool surface it would
read. Ninety per cent of the value is the join, and the join needs no model at all.

## 1.2 Scope

**In scope**

- The finding: its shape, its closed severity vocabulary, and the rule that its cause is measured.
- Derivation from the existing read model and bounded log tails, with no second store.
- The correlations that must be made, and the rule that one cause yields one finding.
- The remedy vocabulary: an existing control, identified stably, or nothing.
- What a diagnostics record may never carry, and the export's paste-safety property.
- Refresh: when the snapshot is computed, and the bound on what that costs.
- The diagnostics view and the support export.

**Out of scope**

- Any language model, prose explanation, or open-ended question answering. A later
  specification may sit an agent on this surface; nothing here infers.
- Durable history and trend analysis. §2.5 states what a later trend feature would need and why
  the retention decision is not taken here.
- Replacing Settings · Local runtime. SPEC-028 T-24 restructures that rail into capability-first
  groups; this snapshot is the join *over* those facts and does not restructure their home.
- New measurements. Every fact a finding rests on is one the product already takes. Where a
  useful finding needs a measurement that does not exist, the finding is not specified.
- Acting. Nothing here mutates anything, in the world or on the machine (R-19).
- Telemetry. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. The export is a file the user chooses to share.

## 1.3 The finding (R-1..R-8)

- **R-1** A finding SHALL be the only unit of the snapshot, and SHALL carry: a stable
  identifier for its kind, a severity, a short title, the measured facts it rests on with the
  source of each, a cause, and a remedy or the explicit absence of one.
  - **WHEN** two runs of the derivation observe the same conditions **THEN** they produce the
    same findings with the same identifiers, so a finding can be referred to across a restart.

- **R-2** Severity SHALL come from one closed vocabulary: `blocking`, `degraded`, `advisory`,
  `unknown`.
  - `blocking` — something the user could otherwise do cannot happen at all.
  - `degraded` — it happens, but partially, slowly, or with a capability missing.
  - `advisory` — nothing is wrong; a choice or a cost is worth knowing about.
  - `unknown` — a measurement could not be made, and the consequence is stated.
  - **AND** no fifth state SHALL be introduced by a caller; a condition that fits none of these
    is a gap in the vocabulary to be resolved here, not in a screen.

- **R-3** `unknown` SHALL mean a measurement could not be taken, never that a known failure is
  being softened.
  - **WHEN** graphics memory cannot be measured **THEN** the finding is `unknown`, states that
    the floor was not checked, and states that dispatch remains permitted (SPEC-021 D15).

- **R-4** A finding's cause SHALL be a measured clause naming the specific thing observed.
  - **AND** no finding SHALL carry a generic cause — *unavailable*, *not working*, *an error
    occurred* — under any circumstance (SPEC-021 R-10).
  - **WHEN** the underlying subsystem already states a reason **THEN** the finding SHALL carry
    that reason rather than paraphrasing it, so the two can never disagree.

- **R-5** A finding's facts SHALL each name where they came from, and SHALL be the values as
  measured rather than as rendered.
  - **WHEN** a figure is shown in the view **THEN** it is formatted from the fact, and the
    export carries the fact.

- **R-6** One cause SHALL yield one finding.
  - **WHEN** an absent engine disables three recipes **THEN** there is one finding about the
    engine, and the recipes are named within it.
  - **WHEN** a full drive blocks two weight components **THEN** there is one finding about the
    drive.
  - **AND** a symptom whose cause is already the subject of another finding SHALL NOT be
    reported separately.

- **R-7** A finding SHALL be able to say that it explains another, and the snapshot SHALL order
  causes before consequences.

- **R-8** The absence of findings SHALL be a stated result, not an empty surface.
  - **WHEN** nothing is wrong **THEN** the snapshot says what it checked and found sound.

## 1.4 Derived, never collected (R-9..R-13)

- **R-9** The snapshot SHALL be computed from state the application already holds, and SHALL
  NOT introduce a second store of any fact it reports.
  - **AND** it SHALL hold no cache that can outlive the state it was derived from.
  - **WHEN** a fact changes **THEN** the next derivation reflects it with no invalidation step.

- **R-10** A finding SHALL NOT be able to disagree with the screen that shows the same fact.
  - **WHEN** Settings states a component is `blocked` with a reason **THEN** a finding about
    that component carries that state and that reason verbatim.

- **R-11** Derivation SHALL be a pure function of its inputs.
  - **AND** it SHALL perform no network request, spawn no process, and write nothing.

- **R-12** Derivation SHALL never fail the caller.
  - **WHEN** a source cannot be read **THEN** that source is reported as unavailable within the
    snapshot, the findings that do not depend on it are still produced, and the ones that do
    become `unknown` with the source named.
  - **AND** a throw inside any single derivation SHALL cost that finding, not the snapshot.

- **R-13** The snapshot SHALL be a contract in `@arke-studio/contracts`, consumed by the view,
  the export, and any later tool surface from one definition.

## 1.5 What it reads (R-14..R-16)

- **R-14** The snapshot SHALL derive from these sources and no others without amendment:
  the application state the read model already publishes — health, environment, probes,
  setup components, ComfyUI engine and recipe readiness, voice runtime, queues, providers and
  their tool state, manifest and routing, spend and ledger, builds and drift — together with
  bounded tails of the operational log and the provider-call record.

- **R-15** Log reads SHALL be bounded and SHALL NOT read a whole file.
  - **AND** the bound SHALL be a property of the derivation, not of the caller, so no surface
    can ask for an unbounded read.

- **R-16** A source that is absent SHALL be distinguished from a source that is empty.
  - **WHEN** no world is open **THEN** the snapshot is still produced: it describes the machine
    and the installation, which exist independently of any world.

## 1.6 The correlations (R-17..R-18)

- **R-17** The snapshot SHALL make at least these joins, each as a single finding:
  - A recipe disabled for missing model files, where the component carrying those files is
    offered — cause is the missing files, remedy is that component's download.
  - A recipe disabled for missing model files, where that component is blocked for want of
    disk — cause is the drive, stating the drive, what is needed and what is free; the recipe
    is a consequence, not a second finding.
  - A recipe disabled for a failed digest — cause is the specific file, remedy is its repair
    (SPEC-028), and the finding states that a retry will not resolve it.
  - An engine that is absent, unreachable, incompatible or failed, with every recipe it
    disables named in one finding.
  - A URL engine with no mapped models folder, where recipes need files — cause is the missing
    mapping, remedy is the mapping control, and the finding states that this is a limit of a
    non-managed engine rather than a fault.
  - Repeated provider faults for one provider inside a window — one finding naming the
    provider, the count and the window, never one finding per fault.
  - A queue lane paused against a credential fault, with the count of work held behind it —
    cause is the credential, and the held jobs are a consequence.
  - Work enqueued against an engine that is not currently answering.
  - Free disk that would not admit a component the user has already asked for.
  - Spend in the current window materially above the previous one, `advisory`, naming the
    model or capability that accounts for the difference.

- **R-18** A required correlation whose inputs are unavailable SHALL produce an `unknown`
  finding naming the missing input, not silence.

## 1.7 The remedy (R-19..R-22)

- **R-19** The snapshot SHALL be read-only. It SHALL NOT perform, schedule or authorize any
  action, in the world or on the machine.
  - **AND** every mutation reachable from a finding SHALL be one the user performs through a
    control that already exists, subject to whatever gate that control already carries.

- **R-20** A remedy SHALL identify an existing control by a stable identifier, or the finding
  SHALL carry no remedy.
  - **AND** a finding SHALL NOT describe a manual filesystem operation where a control exists.
  - **WHEN** no control exists for a condition **THEN** the finding is advisory about the
    condition and says plainly that the product offers no action for it.

- **R-21** A remedy whose control is no longer present SHALL degrade to no remedy with the
  condition still stated, never to a reference that resolves to nothing.

- **R-22** A remedy SHALL name where its control lives, in the product's own words for that
  place, so a finding read in the export can be acted on in the application.

## 1.8 What it may never carry (R-23..R-26)

- **R-23** No diagnostics record SHALL contain an absolute filesystem path (SPEC-021 §2.11).
  - **AND** where a file must be identified, it SHALL be identified as the product identifies
    it elsewhere — a component, a declared relative file, or an opaque instance digest.

- **R-24** No diagnostics record SHALL contain a secret. Every string reaching a finding SHALL
  pass the redaction boundary, including text quoted from a provider (SPEC-008 R-7).
  - **AND** a provider fault quoted into a finding SHALL be the already-redacted record, not a
    second capture of the same text.

- **R-25** No diagnostics record SHALL contain world content — a world, production, character,
  place or take name, a logline, a prompt, or any authored text.
  - **WHEN** a finding concerns work in the queue **THEN** it carries counts, capabilities and
    states, never the subject of that work.

- **R-26** The export SHALL be safe to paste into a public issue by construction, and this
  SHALL be a tested property rather than a convention.

## 1.9 Refresh (R-27..R-29)

- **R-27** The snapshot SHALL be produced on demand, and on the state transitions that could
  change a finding, and at no other time.
  - **AND** it SHALL NOT poll.

- **R-28** Producing the snapshot SHALL NOT block the application, and SHALL NOT delay any
  work in flight.

- **R-29** The snapshot SHALL carry the instant it was derived, and each finding SHALL carry
  when it first became true within this session.
  - **AND** nothing about a finding SHALL be written to disk (§2.5).

## 1.10 The surfaces (R-30..R-34)

- **R-30** One view SHALL present the findings in severity order, causes before consequences,
  each with its remedy where it has one.
  - **AND** it SHALL state plainly when there is nothing to report.
  - **AND** it SHALL NOT restate a fact whose own group already carries it without adding the
    join that made it a finding.

- **R-31** The view's language SHALL be labels, counts, states and refusals. Explanation of why
  a rule exists belongs in this document, not on the screen.

- **R-32** The export SHALL carry the findings and the measured facts beneath them, in a form
  that can be read without the application.

- **R-33** The export SHALL be produced by an explicit action and SHALL be transmitted nowhere.

- **R-34** Neither surface SHALL be a precondition for the other. The findings exist as a
  contract; a build that ships only the export is coherent.

# 2 · Design

## 2.1 Why derived rather than collected

The tempting shape is a diagnostics service that gathers facts on a timer into a record of its
own. It is the wrong shape here for one reason: the moment a fact exists in two places it can
disagree with itself, and a diagnostic that contradicts the screen beside it is worse than no
diagnostic at all — it costs a debugging session to discover the tool is the thing that is
wrong.

Deriving from the read model makes that class of bug unreachable. There is no invalidation, no
staleness window and no second definition of *blocked*. It also makes the derivation trivially
testable: state in, findings out, no clock and no filesystem for anything but the bounded log
tails.

The cost is that the snapshot can only be as good as what the read model publishes. That is
accepted, and R-14's list is deliberately closed: a finding wanting a measurement the product
does not take is an argument for taking that measurement, in the spec that owns it.

## 2.2 The finding vocabulary, and why it is closed

Four severities are enough to order a list and to decide a tone, and a closed set is what makes
the ordering meaningful. The distinction that matters most is `unknown` against `degraded`: an
unmeasurable graphics card and a card that is too small are completely different situations, and
collapsing them was the specific error SPEC-021 D15 exists to prevent. A machine that could not
be measured must still be allowed to try.

`advisory` earns its place separately. A finding that says *you have three recipes and room for
one* is not a fault, and putting it in the same bucket as a broken engine trains the reader to
ignore the surface.

## 2.3 One cause, one finding

This is the whole product, stated as a rule. Settings already shows every symptom; a snapshot
that shows the same symptoms in a different order has done nothing. What the user cannot get
today is *this one is why the other three are red*.

The deduplication has an obligation attached: a consequence that is hidden must still be
reachable. R-7's explanation link is how — a finding names what it explains, so the view can
show the causal chain without listing four equal-looking rows.

## 2.4 The remedy is a control, not an instruction

R-20 is a safety property disguised as a usability rule.

Constraining every remedy to a control the product already has means the snapshot cannot
propose an operation the product cannot perform, cannot invent a destructive suggestion, and
cannot route around the accept gate — because whatever gate the control carries, it still
carries. *Delete these weights to free space* is not expressible unless a delete control exists.

That constraint is what makes this surface safe to put an advisor on later. An agent reading
findings and speaking in the vocabulary of existing controls is bounded by construction; an
agent reading raw state and speaking free prose about the filesystem is not.

## 2.5 History, and why it is not here

The most valuable question this surface cannot answer is *what changed*. **This worked last
week** needs a record over time, and the snapshot deliberately keeps none.

That is a retention decision, not an oversight: how long, where on disk, what is inside it, what
happens when a user shares it, and how it interacts with R-23 through R-25 once findings outlive
the state that produced them. Each of those is a real question, and none of them needs answering
for the join to be worth having.

R-29's in-session `firstSeen` is the deliberate half-measure. It costs nothing, it distinguishes
*this has been true since you opened the app* from *this just happened*, and it commits to
nothing on disk.

## 2.6 Why no model

Every finding in R-17 is arithmetic over facts that are already measured. A language model would
make each of them slower, non-reproducible, untestable and occasionally wrong, in exchange for
nothing — the answers have exactly one correct value and the product already knows it.

The place a model genuinely earns its keep is the open question: *why is this month more
expensive*, *which of these three should I install given how I work*, *is this the same failure
as last time*. Those need judgement, and some of them need the history §2.5 defers.

Building the join first is what makes that later agent cheap and likely to be right: it reads a
small, correct, deduplicated structure instead of scraping four log files and a read model, and
its output vocabulary is already bounded by R-20. If the agent is never built, the join is still
the more useful half.

# 3 · Decisions

**D1 — Derived, never collected (R-9).** Rejected: a diagnostics service with its own store and
a refresh interval. It is the conventional shape and it buys a staleness window, an invalidation
bug class, and a second definition of every state word. The snapshot is worth having only if it
cannot contradict the app.

**D2 — The remedy vocabulary is existing controls (R-20).** The usability argument is that a
finding should be actionable. The real argument is containment: this is the surface an advisor
will eventually sit on, and bounding its action vocabulary now is far cheaper than bounding a
model's prose later. §2.4.

**D3 — One cause, one finding (R-6).** The rail already lists symptoms. Deduplication is the
feature, and without it this ships as a seventh group nobody reads.

**D4 — No model in this specification (out of scope).** Not a rejection of the agent — a
sequencing decision. The deterministic join is most of the value, is testable, and is the
agent's tool surface. Shipping the agent first would mean an LLM scraping raw sources, which is
the fragile version of the same feature. §2.6.

**D5 — Read-only, always (R-19).** Mutations go through the gate. A diagnostic that acts is a
hole in the product's strongest boundary, and the hole would be in the one component whose whole
job is to be trusted about the state of things.

**D6 — No durable history (R-29).** Deferred, not refused. The retention questions are real and
the join is useful without answering them. §2.5.

**D7 — `unknown` is a finding (R-3).** Consistent with SPEC-021 D15. A measurement that could
not be taken is information; suppressing it produces a surface that looks healthy because it is
blind.

**D8 — The export's safety is tested, not conventional (R-26).** A paste-safe artifact whose
safety rests on reviewers remembering a rule is not paste-safe. The property is asserted
directly, because the failure — a key or a path in a public issue — is not recoverable.

# 5 · Issue map

Issues are not yet filed. The proposed decomposition:

| Part | Covers | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| A | The finding contract, the severity vocabulary, and the derivation from read-model state alone | §1.3, §1.4, §1.6, §2.1–§2.3 |
| B | Log-tail sources, the correlations that need them, and the redaction and paste-safety properties | §1.5, §1.7, §1.8, §2.4 |
| C | The diagnostics view | §1.10 |
| D | The support export | §1.9, §1.10 |

Part A ships alone and is useful alone: it is the contract and the arithmetic, testable without
a surface. B extends the source set. C and D are independent consumers of A, and either may ship
without the other (R-34).

A later specification may place a system agent on this surface. It depends on A and B, and on
the history deferred in §2.5 for the subset of questions that need it.
