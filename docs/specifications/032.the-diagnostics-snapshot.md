# 1 · Requirements

## 1.1 Summary

Arke measures a great deal about its own operation and states all of it honestly. The ComfyUI
readiness walk names the first thing that failed rather than reporting a generic unavailable.
Setup names the component, its size, its state and its reason. The environment check carries
free disk, the path budget and the native index. Queue lanes carry their pause, its kind and
the count held behind it. `ClientState.app` holds thirty such fields, and a support bundle
already exists to export a subset of them (SPEC-008 R-6).

Some of these facts are joined already. A ComfyUI recipe row shows its weight component's own
refusal in place of its own reason when the two disagree, so a recipe disabled for want of a
model file reads the disk that refused the download rather than the symptom — SPEC-028 T-25
built that, and Components suppresses what another group already states. Where a join has been
made, it works.

**Joins are made one screen at a time, by hand, and only within one subsystem.** Nothing in the
product crosses from one subsystem to another. The clearest case:

> A job carries the engine it was dispatched against — source, opaque instance digest, process
> epoch (`Job.engine`). The ComfyUI status carries that same instance digest and the engine's
> current state. Six local video jobs can therefore be sitting in the queue against an engine
> that died an hour ago, and **no object in the product holds both halves**. Activity shows six
> jobs. The ComfyUI group shows a failed engine. Neither says the six are going nowhere.

That shape repeats: repeated provider faults are in the operational log and counted nowhere;
spend carries one rolling window and no comparison to the last; free disk is joined to ComfyUI's
weights and to nothing else, so a Voxa model blocked for space says so in a vocabulary its own
group never uses. Every one of these is arithmetic over facts already measured. None of them
is anybody's job.

This specification makes that arithmetic a first-class thing. The unit is a **finding**: a
condition, the facts under it, the cause in one clause, and — where the product has one — the
control that resolves it. The snapshot is the set of findings true right now, derived from state
the app already holds, keeping no store of its own.

It is deliberately the substrate and not an advisor. A system agent answering open questions in
prose is a separate specification, and this is the surface it would read. The join is most of
the value and it needs no model at all.

## 1.2 Scope

**In scope**

- The finding: its identity, its closed severity vocabulary, and the rule that its cause is measured.
- Derivation as a pure function, including how a finding knows when it first became true.
- The correlations that must be made, with the constants that make them reproducible.
- Declared subsumption: how one finding suppresses another, and how a shared consequence is modelled.
- The control registry a remedy points into.
- What a diagnostics record may never carry, and how the export's safety is enforced.
- Refresh: what triggers a derivation, and the bound on what one costs.
- The diagnostics view, and the **extension of the existing support bundle** (amends SPEC-008 R-6).

**Out of scope**

- Any language model, prose explanation, or open-ended question answering.
- Durable history and trend analysis. §2.5 states what a later trend feature needs and why the
  retention decision is not taken here.
- Replacing Settings · Local AI. SPEC-033 restructures that rail — Local runtime became Local
  AI, with the machinery on Engines beside it; this is the join over those facts and does not
  restructure their home. SPEC-033 R-56 makes Local AI a third consumer of this contract, which
  R-40's independence rule is what permits.
- Replacing the joins already made inside a subsystem. The ComfyUI recipe row keeps its own
  behaviour (SPEC-028 T-25); the snapshot does not re-render it.
- New measurements. Where a useful finding needs a measurement the product does not take, the
  finding is not specified. R-16 is not an exception to this: it offers the user the control
  that re-measures, and forbids the snapshot from measuring anything itself.
- Acting. Nothing here mutates anything (R-23).
- Telemetry. Nothing is transmitted. The export is a file the user chooses to share.

## 1.3 The finding (R-1..R-10)

- **R-1** A finding SHALL carry two identities: a **kind**, stable across releases, naming the
  rule that produced it; and an **occurrence**, distinguishing findings of one kind from each
  other.
  - **WHEN** two providers independently satisfy the same rule **THEN** there are two findings of
    one kind with different occurrences, each separately addressable for linking and ordering.
  - **AND** an occurrence SHALL be derived only from values §1.8 permits a record to carry.

- **R-2** A finding SHALL carry: its kind and occurrence, a severity, a short title, the measured
  facts it rests on with the source and measurement time of each, a cause, and a remedy or the
  explicit absence of one.

- **R-3** Severity SHALL come from one closed vocabulary: `blocking`, `degraded`, `advisory`,
  `unknown`, `unmeasured`.
  - `blocking` — something the user could otherwise do cannot happen at all.
  - `degraded` — it happens, but partially, slowly, or with a capability missing.
  - `advisory` — nothing is wrong; a choice or a cost is worth knowing about.
  - `unknown` — a measurement was attempted and failed; the consequence is stated.
  - `unmeasured` — the measurement was never requested; the finding names what would request it.
  - **AND** no further state SHALL be introduced by a caller.

- **R-4** `unknown` and `unmeasured` SHALL be distinct, and neither SHALL ever mean that a known
  failure is being softened.
  - **WHEN** graphics memory was probed and the probe failed **THEN** `unknown`.
  - **WHEN** no probe has been requested this session **THEN** `unmeasured`, naming the control
    that requests one (§2.2 — this is the common case, not the rare one).
  - **AND** both SHALL state that dispatch remains permitted (SPEC-021 D15).

- **R-5** Severity SHALL be a property of the condition alone, and SHALL NOT change because a
  remedy does or does not exist.
  - **WHEN** a blocking condition has no control **THEN** it remains `blocking` and carries no
    remedy. It SHALL NOT be reclassified as `advisory`.

- **R-6** A finding's cause SHALL name the specific thing observed, and SHALL NOT be generic.
  - **WHEN** the subsystem states its own reason **THEN** the finding carries that reason, after
    redaction (R-13), rather than paraphrasing it.
  - **WHEN** the subsystem's own reason is itself generic **THEN** the finding carries it, marks
    itself as carrying an upstream-generic reason, and names the specification that owes a
    better one. It SHALL NOT invent a more specific cause than was measured.

- **R-7** A finding SHALL carry the facts as measured, each naming its source and the instant it
  was measured. Formatting for display SHALL happen at the surface, never in the fact.

- **R-8** Subsumption SHALL be **declared, never inferred**. Each finding kind SHALL declare the
  kinds it subsumes.
  - **AND** a finding SHALL be suppressed from primary presentation only when a finding of a kind
    that declares it is present in the same snapshot.
  - **AND** suppression SHALL remove it from primary presentation only; it remains in the
    snapshot, reachable from every finding that subsumes it.

- **R-9** A consequence SHALL be a finding in its own right, referenced by its causes, and SHALL
  appear once however many causes it has.
  - **WHEN** two independent causes disable one recipe **THEN** there are three findings: two
    causes, one suppressed consequence, and two edges.

- **R-10** The absence of findings SHALL be a stated result naming what was checked, never an
  empty surface.

## 1.4 Derived, never collected (R-11..R-16)

- **R-11** The snapshot SHALL be a pure function of explicit inputs:
  `derive(state, tails, previous, now)` — the application state, the bounded log tails, the
  previous snapshot, and the derivation instant.
  - **AND** it SHALL perform no network request, spawn no process, read no file beyond the
    tails it is given, and write nothing.
  - **AND** identical inputs SHALL produce an identical snapshot, `firstSeen` included.

- **R-12** The snapshot SHALL introduce no store of any fact **about the machine or the
  installation**. Every such fact SHALL be read from where the product already keeps it.
  - **AND** the only state the snapshot carries between derivations is its own bookkeeping —
    the first-observation instant per occurrence — which is an input under R-11 and belongs to
    the caller, not to the derivation.

- **R-13** A finding SHALL NOT be able to disagree with the screen that shows the same fact.
  - **AND** where a subsystem states a reason, redaction (§1.8) takes precedence over verbatim
    carriage: the finding carries the reason **as it survives the redaction boundary**. Where
    redaction alters it, the finding says so.

- **R-14** Derivation SHALL never fail its caller.
  - **WHEN** a source cannot be read **THEN** that source is named unavailable in the snapshot,
    findings not depending on it are still produced, and those that do become `unknown`.
  - **WHEN** a single rule throws **THEN** the snapshot carries a finding of a reserved kind
    naming the rule's kind and the error's type — never its message, which is unvouched text —
    and every other rule still runs.

- **R-15** The snapshot SHALL be a contract in `@arke-studio/contracts`, consumed by the view,
  the export and any later tool surface from one definition.

- **R-16** A fact SHALL carry when it was measured, and a finding resting on a fact older than
  the staleness bound — **fifteen minutes**, measured backwards from the derivation instant —
  SHALL say so and offer the existing control that re-measures it.
  - **AND** the snapshot SHALL NOT itself trigger a measurement (R-11).
  - **WHEN** free disk was measured at start-up and the drive has since been emptied **THEN**
    the finding states the figure with its age rather than asserting a current shortage.

## 1.5 What it reads (R-17..R-19)

- **R-17** The snapshot SHALL derive from these sources and no others without amendment:
  from the published application state — `version`, `health`, `env`, `runtime`, `harness`,
  `harnessInfo`, `setup`, `comfyui`, `voiceRuntime`, `queues`, `jobs`, `providers`,
  `providerTools`, `manifest`, `routing`, `models`, `spend`, `ledger`, `drift`, `builds`,
  `update` — together with a bounded tail of the operational log.
  - **AND** the provider-call record SHALL NOT be a source. It holds request and response
    payloads — prompts and world content — and is deliberately excluded from support
    diagnostics; nothing here reverses that.
  - **AND** the `ledger` source SHALL carry whether the read that produced it failed. It is
    built from a file, so an unreadable file must be distinguishable from an empty one —
    R-19's distinction, which R-21 consumes; folded into an empty array, a failed read is a
    clean fortnight.

- **R-18** The operational log tail SHALL be bounded at **500 records**, the bound SHALL be a
  property of the derivation rather than a caller argument, and the read SHALL NOT load the
  whole file to obtain them.
  - **AND** the existing tail helper reads the file whole and takes its bound from the caller;
    correcting that is work this specification owns (§5, Part B).

- **R-19** A source that is absent SHALL be distinguished from one that is empty.
  - **WHEN** no world is open **THEN** the snapshot is still produced: it describes the machine
    and the installation, which exist independently of any world.

## 1.6 The correlations (R-20..R-22)

- **R-20** The snapshot SHALL make at least these joins. Each names its inputs, and each is
  reproducible because its constants are stated here rather than chosen by an implementer.

  1. **Work against a dead engine.** Non-terminal jobs whose frozen engine identity matches the
     currently resolved engine, where that engine is not `ready`. Cause is the engine; the held
     work is a count, never its subject.
  2. **A lane paused on a credential.** A queue lane with `pauseKind: "credential"` and a
     non-zero held count. Cause is the credential; the held count is a consequence.
  3. **A recipe short of disk.** A disabled recipe whose weight component is blocked for space —
     cause is the volume, stating it, what is needed and what is free.
  4. **Any component short of disk.** The same join for every other component the user has
     asked for, which no screen makes today.
  5. **A recipe short of files.** A disabled recipe whose weight component is offered — remedy
     is that component's download.
  6. **A recipe with a failed digest** — cause names the file, remedy is repair, and the finding
     states that a retry will not resolve it.
  7. **An engine that is absent, unreachable, incompatible or failed**, subsuming every recipe
     it disables.
  8. **A URL engine with no mapped models folder** where recipes need files — stated as a limit
     of a non-managed engine, not a fault.
  9. **Repeated provider faults.** **Three or more** provider fault records for one provider
     within **fifteen minutes**, counted from the operational log tail, the window measured
     backwards from the derivation instant. One finding per provider carrying the count and the
     window — never one per fault.
  10. **Spend above the previous period.** The trailing **seven days** against the seven before
      it, computed from the ledger. Advisory when the later period exceeds the earlier by **both
      fifty per cent and 1,000,000 microUSD**, the floor being what stops a rounding difference
      on a quiet fortnight reading as a trend. It names the model accounting for the largest
      share of the difference, and where two models account for an equal share it names them in
      manifest order — a tie is rare and a coin toss would make two conforming builds disagree.

- **R-21** A required correlation whose inputs cannot be read SHALL produce an `unknown` finding
  naming the missing input.
  - **AND** an input that is **legitimately absent** rather than unreadable SHALL produce no
    finding at all — the distinction R-19 draws, carried into the correlations.
  - **WHEN** an installation is four days old and has no previous spend period **THEN** there is
    no spend finding, and no `unknown` either.

- **R-22** A component in a transient state — queued, downloading or installing — SHALL NOT be a
  fault.
  - **AND** it SHALL produce a finding only where it blocks something else, and then as
    `advisory` naming what is waiting.

## 1.7 The remedy (R-23..R-27)

- **R-23** The snapshot SHALL be read-only. It SHALL NOT perform, schedule or authorize any
  action, in the world or on the machine.
  - **AND** every mutation reachable from a finding SHALL be one the user performs through an
    existing control, subject to whatever gate that control already carries.

- **R-24** A remedy SHALL identify a control through a **registry declared in contracts** and
  read by both the derivation and the view.
  - **AND** the registry SHALL name, for each control, where it lives in the product's own words
    for that place.
  - **AND** a control removed from the product SHALL be removed from the registry, so a remedy
    naming it fails to build rather than dangling at runtime (§2.4).

- **R-25** A finding whose condition has no control SHALL carry no remedy, keep its severity
  (R-5), and say plainly that the product offers no action for it.

- **R-26** A remedy SHALL NOT describe a manual filesystem operation where a control exists.

- **R-27** The registry SHALL be part of the finding contract, so a build carrying findings
  without a view still resolves every remedy it names.

## 1.8 What it may never carry (R-28..R-32)

- **R-28** No diagnostics record SHALL contain an absolute filesystem path.
  - This is a **new** rule. SPEC-008 R-6 governs credentials in a diagnostics bundle and is
    silent on paths; SPEC-002 R-24 governs world files. Neither covers this, and the existing
    bundle achieves it by construction rather than by rule (§2.6).
  - **AND** where a file or a volume must be identified, it SHALL be identified as a component,
    a declared relative file, an opaque instance digest, or a **volume root** — the last being
    what a disk finding needs to name the drive (R-20.3).

- **R-29** No diagnostics record SHALL contain a secret. Every string SHALL pass the redaction
  boundary, including text quoted from a subsystem or a provider, and redaction SHALL take
  precedence over verbatim carriage (R-13).

- **R-30** No diagnostics record SHALL contain world content — a world, production, character,
  place or take name, a logline, a prompt, or any authored text.
  - **AND** ledger entries carry a world and production identifier; the projection that reaches
    a finding SHALL drop them.

- **R-31** The export SHALL be built from an **enumerated field set**: a field reaches it because
  it was named, never because it was not excluded. Adding a field is the reviewed act.
  - This is what makes the guarantee real, and it is the shape the existing bundle already has.

- **R-32** The export's safety SHALL be asserted by a property test quantifying over generated
  application state containing a registered secret, an absolute path and world content, asserting
  that none of the three appears in the serialised output.
  - **AND** free text admitted under R-13 SHALL be the one place this property cannot be
    established by enumeration alone, and SHALL therefore pass redaction before enumeration.

## 1.9 Refresh (R-33..R-35)

- **R-33** A derivation SHALL be produced on demand, and when any source in R-17 changes.
  - **AND** changes arriving in one tick SHALL be coalesced into **at most one derivation per
    tick**, which is the bound that distinguishes this from polling.
  - **AND** it SHALL NOT run on a timer.

- **R-34** A derivation SHALL NOT run inside a frame handler or on any path that a user action
  awaits, and SHALL NOT delay work in flight.

- **R-35** The snapshot SHALL carry the instant it was derived, and each occurrence SHALL carry
  when it first became true within this session, taken from the previous snapshot under R-11.
  - **AND** nothing SHALL be written to disk (§2.5).

## 1.10 The surfaces (R-36..R-40)

- **R-36** One view SHALL present findings in severity order, causes before consequences, each
  with its remedy where it has one.
  - **AND** suppressed consequences SHALL be reachable from their causes, never listed as peers.
  - **AND** it SHALL state plainly when there is nothing to report.

- **R-37** The view's language SHALL be labels, counts, states and refusals. Why a rule exists
  belongs in this document.

- **R-38** The support bundle of SPEC-008 R-6 SHALL be **extended** to carry the findings
  alongside the fields it already exports. A second export SHALL NOT be built beside it.
  - **AND** its existing action, its redaction on the way out and its transmission to nowhere
    are unchanged.
  - **AND** SPEC-008 R-6 is amended only in what the bundle contains, never in what it forbids.

- **R-39** The bundle SHALL remain readable without the application.

- **R-40** Neither surface SHALL be a precondition for the other, and the findings contract SHALL
  stand without either.

# 2 · Design

## 2.1 Why derived rather than collected

The tempting shape is a diagnostics service gathering facts on a timer into a record of its own.
It is wrong here for one reason: the moment a fact lives in two places it can disagree with
itself, and a diagnostic that contradicts the screen beside it is worse than none — it costs a
debugging session to discover the tool is the thing that is wrong.

Deriving removes that class of bug entirely. There is no invalidation, no staleness window and no
second definition of *blocked*.

R-12 states the prohibition precisely, because the loose version of it was wrong. The snapshot
does keep one thing between derivations: when each occurrence was first seen. That is not a fact
about the machine — it is the snapshot's own bookkeeping, it exists nowhere else to be read from,
and no screen can contradict it. R-11 keeps the derivation pure by making it an input, so the
store belongs to the caller and the function still satisfies *same inputs, same output*.

The cost is that the snapshot can only be as good as what the read model publishes. R-17's list
is closed for that reason: a finding wanting a measurement the product does not take is an
argument for taking it, in the specification that owns it.

## 2.2 Why `unmeasured` exists

Two of Arke's most useful measurements are not taken until someone asks. `app.runtime` — which
carries graphics memory, the figure every hardware gate turns on — is null until **Settings ·
Local AI** mounts and requests detection, because detection costs a subprocess and is not done on
every boot. `app.harness` is the same and says so.

*(Amended by SPEC-033 R-59. That screen was Settings · Local runtime when this was written, and
R-46 renamed it. The control registry R-24 requires moves with the prose: a registry naming a
route that no longer exists is exactly the dangling remedy §2.4 exists to make impossible, and it
is invisible until somebody follows it.)*

So in a session where the user never opened that screen — which is most sessions, and precisely
the session in which somebody reaches for diagnostics — every hardware fact is missing. Reporting
that as `unknown` would say *we tried and failed* when the truth is *nobody asked*, and it would
make a surface that is blind by default look like a surface that is broken.

`unmeasured` says which it is and names the control that fixes it. The snapshot does not request
the measurement itself: that would spawn a process from inside a derivation R-11 requires to be
pure, and would make opening a diagnostics view mutate the state it reports on.

## 2.3 Declared subsumption

R-8 is the whole product stated as a rule, and the first draft of it did not work.

Saying *one cause, one finding* describes a relation the derivation would have to discover, and
nothing in the data says that a full drive explains a disabled recipe. The only thing that knows
is the rule that made the join — which means the relation is **declared**, and R-20's list is
the declaration.

The consequence must also be a node rather than a sentence inside its parent. Two independent
causes can disable one recipe; a consequence modelled as text inside one cause cannot be attached
to both and still reported once. R-9 makes it a finding with edges, which costs a suppression
concept and buys an expressible causal graph.

## 2.4 The remedy is a control, not an instruction

R-24 is a safety property disguised as a usability rule.

Constraining every remedy to a control the product already has means the snapshot cannot propose
an operation the product cannot perform, cannot invent a destructive one, and cannot route around
the accept gate — whatever gate the control carries, it still carries. *Delete these weights to
free space* is not expressible unless a delete control exists.

The registry has to be a contract rather than a convention because the derivation lives in
`@arke-studio/contracts` and the controls live in the renderer: a derivation cannot observe that a
button was deleted. Making the identifiers a declared set both sides read turns a dangling remedy
into a build failure — the only place to catch it, since a dangling remedy is invisible until
someone follows it.

Nothing like that registry exists today. The nearest things are settings route slugs and the six
runtime group ids that ride in the query string, both a level or two coarser than *Repair* or
*Map a folder*. Building it is real work, and §5 assigns it.

## 2.5 History, and why it is not here

The most valuable question this surface cannot answer is *what changed*. **This worked last week**
needs a record over time, and the snapshot keeps none.

That is a retention decision, not an oversight: how long, where, what is inside it, what happens
when it is shared, and how it interacts with §1.8 once findings outlive the state that produced
them.

R-35's in-session `firstSeen` is the deliberate half-measure. It distinguishes *true since you
opened the app* from *just happened*, and commits nothing to disk.

Note that the windowed correlations in R-20 do **not** need history. Provider faults are countable
from the operational log, which already carries timestamps; the previous spend period is
computable from the ledger, which arrives whole — and arrives saying when its read failed, so an
unreadable file is an `unknown` rather than a quiet fortnight (R-21). Neither asks the snapshot
to remember anything.

## 2.6 The export already exists

`buildDiagnosticsBundle` ships today for SPEC-008 R-6 and already does most of what a second
export would: an explicit user action, transmitted nowhere, redacted on the way out, with a
bounded log tail and an enumerated field set carrying the comment *"Deliberately absent: worlds,
world names, sheets, canon, productions, file paths."*

R-38 extends it rather than replacing it, and the frontmatter declares `amends: [SPEC-008]`
accordingly. Building a second export beside the first would be exactly the failure D1 exists to
prevent, applied to the export instead of the store.

It also settles what R-31 and R-32 should say. Paste-safety cannot be a property of the redaction
boundary: that is a denylist of secrets the registry happened to see this session plus a
field-name heuristic, and a secret it never saw in a field not named like a credential passes
straight through. It *can* be a property of an enumerated field set — which the existing bundle
already is. The honest requirement is therefore about enumeration, with redaction as the second
line for the one thing enumeration cannot vouch for: free text quoted from another subsystem.

## 2.7 Why no model

Every correlation in R-20 is arithmetic over facts already measured. A language model would make
each slower, non-reproducible, untestable and occasionally wrong, in exchange for nothing.

One correlation is a partial exception worth naming: spend. `SpendStatus` carries one rolling
window and no comparison, so R-20.10 is a fresh computation over the ledger with a period
boundary and an attribution, and *materially above* was a judgement until R-20.10 replaced it
with two numbers. Those numbers are arguable, which is why they are in the specification where
they can be argued with, rather than in an implementation where they would be discovered.

The place a model genuinely earns its keep is the open question: *why is this month more
expensive*, *which of these should I install given how I work*, *is this the same failure as last
time*. Building the join first makes that agent cheap and likely to be right — it reads a small,
correct, deduplicated structure instead of scraping four sources, and R-24 has already bounded
what it can propose.

# 3 · Decisions

**D1 — Derived, never collected (R-11, R-12).** Rejected: a diagnostics service with its own
store and a refresh interval. It buys a staleness window, an invalidation bug class and a second
definition of every state word. §2.1.

**D2 — `firstSeen` is an input, not a store (R-11).** The first draft made it a property the
derivation somehow knew, which contradicted its own purity rule and made two matrix rows
unsatisfiable together. Passing the previous snapshot in costs a parameter and keeps both.

**D3 — Subsumption is declared (R-8).** A discovered causal relation is not available in the
data. R-20's joins are the declaration, and suppression happens on a declared edge.

**D4 — A consequence is a node (R-9).** Costs a suppression concept. Buys the two-causes-one-
recipe case, which text-inside-a-parent cannot express. §2.3.

**D5 — Five severities, not four (R-3, R-4).** `unmeasured` was added because the common case —
nobody has opened the screen that triggers hardware detection — is neither a failure nor a
healthy silence, and calling it `unknown` misreports the most frequent state the surface will be
in. §2.2.

**D6 — Severity is independent of remedy (R-5).** A blocking condition with no control is still
blocking. Downgrading it to advisory would put a dead engine in the same bucket as a cost note.

**D7 — Redaction beats verbatim (R-13, R-29).** Subsystem reasons are built from subprocess
output and `Error.message`, which routinely embed absolute paths. Carrying them verbatim would
make R-13 defeat §1.8 on the most common failure path.

**D8 — Extend the existing bundle (R-38).** Rejected: a new export. A second artifact answering
the same question is the same failure D1 names, and the shipped one already has the enumeration
property R-31 wants. §2.6.

**D9 — Paste-safety by enumeration (R-31, R-32).** The redaction boundary is two heuristics, not
a construction. An enumerated field set is enforceable and reviewable; the property test asserts
it rather than three examples standing in for it.

**D10 — No model in this specification.** Sequencing, not rejection. The deterministic join is
most of the value, is testable, and is the agent's tool surface. §2.7.

**D11 — Read-only, always (R-23).** Mutations go through the gate. A diagnostic that acts is a
hole in the product's strongest boundary, in the one component whose job is to be trusted about
the state of things.

**D12 — No durable history (R-35).** Deferred, not refused. §2.5.

**D13 — The correlation constants are in the spec (R-20).** Three faults in fifteen minutes,
seven days against seven, fifty per cent and a floor, and the fifteen-minute staleness bound
(R-16). Arguable numbers stated where they can be argued with; an implementer choosing them
privately makes two conforming builds disagree.

# 4 · Adversarial test matrix

| # | Scenario | Expected | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Six non-terminal jobs against an engine that has since failed | One `blocking` finding; the engine is the cause, the six a count; no job subject in the record | R-20.1, R-30 |
| 2 | Lane paused on a credential, work held | One finding; cause is the credential; held count is a consequence | R-20.2 |
| 3 | Weights missing, mapped drive full | One finding naming the volume and both figures; the recipe is a suppressed consequence | R-20.3, R-8 |
