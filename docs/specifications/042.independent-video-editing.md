# 1 · Requirements

## 1.1 Summary

The editor is a lightweight video editor that also consumes generated takes. Scenes and shots
are optional source metadata and assembly inputs. A production with no scenes can import personal
footage, place it on Picture, detach sound and independently cut and move both. The saved timeline
remains the authority for preview and delivery.

## 1.2 Import and placement

- **R-1** Import media SHALL be available in the editor without creating a scene or shot. Import
  to timeline SHALL append selected videos/images sequentially to Picture, and audio files to an
  audio track. Library-only import SHALL file and add assets without placing clips.
- **R-2** Import SHALL use world artifact filing, copying and measuring files and deduplicating
  identical content. Every successfully filed asset SHALL be added to the current Library when
  its timeline edit succeeds. The source file may subsequently move without breaking playback.
- **R-3** Desktop drops into the Library SHALL import there. Drops onto Picture SHALL file and
  place picture at the requested frame. The renderer SHALL pass File objects through the trusted
  preload; resolved host paths SHALL never enter timeline sources or renderer-visible state.
- **R-4** Multiple files SHALL retain picker/drop order. A cancelled picker SHALL write nothing.
  Partial filing failure SHALL identify failed files. Import SHALL preserve successfully filed
  world media if the timeline revision changes during copying; it SHALL report the refusal and
  allow recovery through the Library picker, never replay the placement against a different cut.
- **R-5** Library picture media SHALL offer an explicit append to main Picture action and an
  overlay action. A deliberate drop SHALL preserve its target frame or visibly refuse overlap;
  it SHALL NOT silently move the clip past occupied regions.

## 1.3 Independent clips and audio

- **R-6** Picture clips SHALL show their source labels, including imported filenames. The
  timeline SHALL draw editable clip boundaries. Scene bands SHALL be optional, off by default;
  story assembly remains available without making scene boundaries structural editing limits.
- **R-7** Detach audio SHALL be available for a selected video with measured embedded audio. In
  one timeline command batch it SHALL create an audio track if needed, place an independent audio
  clip with matching timeline start, duration and audible physical source range, and mute the
  picture clip. The range SHALL include any accepted-selection trim and pass-segment offset;
  copying a raw clip offset is insufficient when the source resolver adds either. This references
  the existing media rather than generating a second file.
- **R-8** Detachment SHALL explain refusal for absent, silent or unmeasured media, and prevent a
  repeated detachment while the picture is already muted. A generated shot's detached sound SHALL
  cite the specific take heard at detachment, so a later accepted-take change cannot replace it.
- **R-9** Imported picture and audio detached under R-7 SHALL independently support move, trim, split, duplicate,
  delete and ripple delete through the same timeline algebra and durable history. Editing one
  half SHALL NOT move or delete the other. Trims SHALL respect measured source limits.
- **R-10** Preview and export SHALL consume the same edited source ranges and sound policy. The
  detached track supplies the sound exactly once; muted picture contributes none. Saved edits
  SHALL survive world reopen and support undo/redo, including atomic detachment.

## 1.4 Compatibility and scope

- **R-11** Existing saved timelines, story assembly, generation and immutable takes SHALL remain
  compatible. No migration SHALL create artificial scenes or shots for imported footage.
- **R-12** Import and timeline errors SHALL be visible in the editor. Concurrent edits SHALL
  retain SPEC-037 revision fencing and world ownership checks.
- **R-13** The editor SHALL preserve reviewed `performance` sources introduced by #115: immutable
  identity/hash, exact physical source range, lead-in, timing intent and atomic paired-overlap
  placement. These sources remain visibly tied to a shot slot and use the existing reviewed
  placement action for retiming. Import/detachment SHALL NOT convert them to ordinary artifacts,
  bypass the timeline write service's validation callback or round their exact playback/export
  ranges to the frame windows drawn by the editor. These constraints SHALL NOT apply to ordinary
  imported/detached artifact or take audio merely because it sits on a Dialogue track.
- **R-14** Moving, trimming, splitting, duplicating or deleting a shot slot SHALL NOT silently
  retime its reviewed performances. Missing, ambiguous or stale reviewed placements SHALL be
  identified in the editor with the existing reviewed-placement recovery, preserving the shared
  render-plan refusal until repaired. Scene-band visibility SHALL NOT affect slot calculation.
- **R-15** A shot Picture clip's optional `performanceSourceClipId` and `set-performance-source`
  command from #256 SHALL remain compatible with timeline history. This is a generation soundtrack
  reference, not a linked editing group. Master-source time SHALL remain Music source-in plus
  Picture start minus Music start; Picture media trim SHALL NOT shift it. Detach audio extracts
  embedded video sound only: it SHALL NOT copy or mute an external master track, duplicate a
  reviewed performance, or copy the Picture-only reference onto a new audio clip.
- **R-16** Edits affecting a master binding SHALL preserve current hash/revision and range checks
  before preparing or enqueueing new generation. Missing, split, non-covering or ambiguous sources
  SHALL produce a visible reference problem without choosing another clip automatically. Already
  queued work SHALL retain its frozen prepared audio; ordinary playback of an imported clip SHALL
  NOT acquire a generation-reference prerequisite.
- **R-17** New audio tracks SHALL be ordinary, renameable Audio tracks, numbered as created.
  Empty Dialogue, Ambience and Music lanes SHALL NOT be displayed by default. Tracks appear when
  explicitly added or needed by placement/detachment. Any audio track may hold any playable sound.
- **R-18** Audio clips SHALL have an optional role: Unspecified, Voice, Music or Ambience.
  New clips default to Unspecified and neither trigger nor receive automatic speech ducking.
  Voice defines speech windows; Music and Ambience receive configured ducking. Existing clips
  without a role inherit their legacy track kind so reopening does not change the mix.
- **R-19** An audio track MAY have a default role for future placements. Placement records that
  default on the new clip. Changing a default SHALL NOT retag existing clips; a clip's explicit
  role overrides it. Role/default edits SHALL use ordinary durable commands and undo/redo.
- **R-20** Roles SHALL be selectable in the Inspector after placement, independent of track
  location. Transcription targets Voice clips. Reviewed performance sources retain Voice behavior
  and their reviewed placement safeguards on any audio track. New detached sound starts Unspecified
  unless the destination track has an explicitly chosen default.
- **R-21** Master synchronization SHALL reference an artifact clip on any audio track, independent
  of role or lane name. The #256 identity, coverage, physical-range and generation revision checks
  remain unchanged. The references to Music in R-15 describe the legacy source, not a new restriction.

This increment covers independent clip editing, not CapCut feature parity. Transitions, effects,
speed changes, automatic beat edits, persistent linked groups and ripple insert/overwrite modes
are outside this increment. Detach means the clips become independent immediately. Converting a
reviewed performance into unrestricted editing media is also outside this increment; its existing
timing/provenance guarantees remain explicit rather than disappearing behind ordinary controls.

# 2 · Design

Reuse artifact filing and the production timeline; introduce no second asset database or editor
store. Import is a bounded coordinator operation: file and measure, then commit one fenced
timeline batch. Files are durable library material even when placement is refused. Shared pure
helpers build import/placement and detachment commands; both the UI and coordinator use the
existing command validation, history and render planning.

Scene bands remain a view preference. A clip may retain shot provenance while its trim and
position belong to the edit. Generated audio is frozen to a take when detached, whereas picture
may continue to resolve its accepted take under existing semantics.

## 2.1 Audio foundation integration baseline

Reviewed against `origin/codex/issue-117-audio-foundation` at
`4e696b5d86b5af4cd78f2dc397840ec611076d01`, fetched 2026-09-05. At review time this work was pushed
but not included in the editor worktree's remote-main base (`9fbf1bd456dbea1a2dff9fed777b357cdda6ebf8`).
The audio foundation subsequently landed through PR #856. The editor worktree was rebased onto
fetched remote main at `d129c493` on 2026-09-05, incorporating the shared contracts, scanning,
audio services and tests together. The historical source links below identify the reviewed
behavior; implementation must use the integrated worktree rather than copying those older files.

The integration boundaries are:

- [Timeline contracts](https://github.com/michaeljosiah/ArkeStudio/blob/4e696b5d86b5af4cd78f2dc397840ec611076d01/packages/contracts/src/timeline.ts):
  preserve the `performance` source, `performanceSourceClipId`, optional timeline hash,
  `set-performance-source`, source-kind validation and measured performance lengths.
- [Dialogue timing](https://github.com/michaeljosiah/ArkeStudio/blob/4e696b5d86b5af4cd78f2dc397840ec611076d01/packages/contracts/src/dialogue-timing.ts)
  derives slots from saved base Picture shot clips before legacy spine/authored fallbacks. Artifact
  clips are valid editor footage without becoming synthetic dialogue slots.
- [Performance placement](https://github.com/michaeljosiah/ArkeStudio/blob/4e696b5d86b5af4cd78f2dc397840ec611076d01/packages/coordinator/src/audio/performance-placement.ts)
  uses `applyTimelineCommand` with in-gate checks on timeline/selection hashes, accepted source
  identity and bytes, current target/voice and mutual overlaps. The import writer must reuse the
  ordinary artifact path without weakening that performance-specific entry point.
- [Render planning](https://github.com/michaeljosiah/ArkeStudio/blob/4e696b5d86b5af4cd78f2dc397840ec611076d01/packages/contracts/src/render-plan.ts)
  retains exact performance seconds as distinct from rounded clip frames and detects stale timing.
- [Master binding](https://github.com/michaeljosiah/ArkeStudio/blob/4e696b5d86b5af4cd78f2dc397840ec611076d01/packages/contracts/src/audio-reference.ts)
  maps timeline placement into the Music clip's physical source. It does not extract a video's
  embedded audio and does not move Picture and Music as a linked pair.

# 3 · Validation

- **T-1** Zero scenes: import two measured videos, verify sequential Picture placement, Library
  membership and filenames, detach one, split/trim/move the audio independently, then export.
- **T-2** Detach a trimmed video: start, duration and source offset match; no doubled audio in the
  shared render plan; undo removes sound and restores picture audio, redo restores the pair.
- **T-3** File deduplication, cancellation, partial failures and a revision change during import
  preserve existing edits and return actionable status.
- **T-4** Persist, reopen and verify imported and detached clips; missing/silent/unmeasured media
  and overlapping placements refuse without a partial timeline write.
- **T-5** Desktop drops resolve paths only in preload; files without a host path are reported.
  Existing library drags and keyboard editing remain operable.
- **T-6** Import, edit and detach personal footage in a production containing reviewed performance
  audio. Untouched performance identities, timing intents, overlap approvals and exact export
  ranges survive persistence and undo/redo. Ordinary Dialogue-track artifact audio stays free to edit.
- **T-7** Change/split/remove a Picture slot supporting reviewed performance audio: the editor
  reports stale/missing/ambiguous placement and the reviewed action repairs it; generic placement
  cannot bypass validation or leave half of a paired-overlap operation committed.
- **T-8** Preserve master bindings through unrelated import/detachment and undo/redo. Verify Music
  source-in and timeline offsets, excluding Picture media trim; moving/splitting/deleting the
  referenced clips invalidates affected new preparation/dispatch as appropriate while queued
  prepared audio stays frozen. Detachment never duplicates the external soundtrack.
- **T-9** After integrating the foundation branch, run its `dialogue-timing.test.ts`, coordinator
  `audio/performances.test.ts` and `audio/master-reference.test.ts` alongside the new editor
  regressions. Existing branch tests were inspected during this review, not executed here.
- **T-10** New generic tracks start without a role; clip overrides and future-only track defaults
  survive reopen and undo/redo. Legacy mixes stay unchanged. Voice, Music and Unspecified clips
  on the same track retain distinct ducking and transcription behavior. A generic audio track
  supports master references and reviewed performances without changing their safeguards.
