# 1 · Requirements

## 1.1 Summary

This specification is the implementation authority for epic #393 Programme D4 (issue #391;
implementation #402). It defines the durable scene-dispatch plan: a first-class aggregate that
records what a scene dispatch is going to spend, holds the compiled passes of issue #398 as its
exact-sent authority, orders passes behind the boundary frames of issue #154, and survives any
crash at any instant with a recovery that is a fold over journals, never a timer or an open
screen.

Today's whole-scene dispatch composes every pass and enqueues them all eagerly. That is correct
only while passes are independent. The moment pass 2 opens on a frame cut from pass 1's landed
clip, three things stop being representable: an authorization that covers the whole scene while
jobs are created one at a time; a pass that cannot be fully materialised until an asset that
does not exist yet is durable; and a restart that must know, from disk alone, which passes ran,
which may run, and which must never run again. The plan is the record that makes those three
representable.

Two boundaries arrive settled and are restated as constraints, not reopened:

1. **A boundary frame is a durable image artifact** (issue #154): bytes in `artifacts/`, a
   sidecar with `sha256:` hash and `boundaryExtraction` provenance (source take, moment,
   method). A video take id is never decoded as a still, anywhere, for any reason.
2. **The compiled pass is the one dispatch object** (issue #398): route resolved first, exact
   wire params, ordered references with roles and frozen versions, named drops, sources. The
   plan persists compiled passes; it does not re-derive prompts, orders, or estimates.

## 1.2 Scope

**In scope:** the plan aggregate and its storage; atomic, idempotent creation; aggregate
authorization math and the two continuation policies; the pass dependency DAG and late
materialisation behind durable boundary frames; the exact-sent record and its linkage to jobs
and takes; the recovery state machine, including queue-batch partial success; cancellation and
terminal outcomes; privacy, retention, and compatibility.

**Out of scope:** the queue's own submission protocol, retries, reconciliation of unwitnessed
submissions, and cost capture (SPEC-009 owns them, unchanged); take finalization and pass
segmentation (SPEC-013); extension/continuation of footage (SPEC-019 §2.13 — a plan may order
passes behind frames, but continuing a clip's motion remains one-hop and take-scoped); the
review UI's visual design.

## 1.3 The aggregate (R-1..R-8)

- **R-1** A plan SHALL be identified by `pl_<ULID>` and stored at
  `productions/<productionId>/plans/<planId>.json` — one aggregate file, written atomically
  once, never edited after authorization. Progress lives in a sibling journal (R-9), so the
  aggregate's bytes are the authorization record forever.
- **R-2** The aggregate SHALL record: `planId`; `requestId` (creation idempotency, R-12);
  `worldId`, `productionId`, `sceneId`; `mode` (`per-shot` | `whole-scene`); the **sources**
  frozen at compile — `canonRevision`, `artDirectionVersion`, `sceneVersion`, every cited
  sheet's version, the production `aspect`, and the `modelId` — exactly as issue #398's
  compiler emitted them; the **policy** (R-16); the **aggregate cap** in micro-USD (R-15); the
  ordered **pass records** (R-4); and `createdAt`.
- **R-3** The aggregate SHALL NOT record mutable status of any kind. A field that changes does
  not belong in a file whose immutability is the authorization's integrity.
- **R-4** Each pass record SHALL be the issue #398 `CompiledPass`, plus: a stable
  `passIndex` (its position, 0-based); a pre-allocated **idempotency key** (ULID, minted at
  plan creation so a crash between materialisation and enqueue re-materialises the same
  submission, per SPEC-009 R-4); and its **dependencies** (R-5).
- **R-5** Dependencies SHALL be declared as
  `dependsOn: [{ passIndex, needs: "boundary-frame" | "completion" }]`. `boundary-frame`
  means: the named pass's primary take has landed, and a boundary still cut from it is a
  durable image artifact this pass's params will carry. `completion` means the named pass
  reached `succeeded` (R-21). The DAG SHALL be acyclic and SHALL only reference lower pass
  indices — a plan is an ordered list with back-references, never a general graph.
- **R-6** A pass whose frame is late-bound SHALL be compiled with everything except the frame
  fields (`references`, `startFrame`, `frameArtifact`, `taskMode`, `route`), and SHALL declare
  the route it will take (`frame`) so its duration and estimate are priced on that route at
  authorization time. The frame fields are bound at materialisation (R-19) and recorded in the
  journal — the aggregate stays immutable.
- **R-7** After authorization the following are immutable and any change forces a **new plan**
  with fresh confirmation: the model and every route, the compiled prompts and reference
  orders, the pass list and DAG, the policy, the cap, and every source version. A plan whose
  scene, sheets, canon, art direction, or production aspect has since moved is **stale**
  (R-24): already-running passes finish, nothing further materialises.
  A production style override is frozen in the compiled pass's existing open `params` bag rather
  than widening this strict aggregate schema; changing or removing it has the same stale outcome.
- **R-8** Plans record spends; they are never deleted by the application. Cancellation and
  terminal outcomes are journal events (R-9). A superseding plan MAY record `supersedes:
  <planId>`, exactly as artifacts do.

## 1.4 The journal and the fold (R-9..R-11)

- **R-9** Progress SHALL live at `productions/<productionId>/plans/<planId>.events.jsonl` —
  append-only, one JSON event per line, fsynced before the action it authorises (SPEC-009's
  outbox discipline, D1). Event kinds: `authorized`, `pass-materialised`, `pass-enqueued`,
  `pass-succeeded`, `pass-failed`, `boundary-extracted`, `boundary-failed`, `continue-approved`,
  `reconfirm-required`, `reconfirmed`, `cancelled`, `stale`, `completed`.
- **R-10** Plan state SHALL be a pure, idempotent fold over (aggregate, events, and the
  SPEC-009 queue journal joined by `jobId`). Replaying the same events yields the same state;
  an event applied twice changes nothing; unknown event kinds are preserved and ignored. No
  correctness may depend on an in-memory timer, a subscription, or an open screen.
- **R-11** Every event SHALL carry `ts`, `planId`, and (where pass-scoped) `passIndex`.
  `pass-enqueued` SHALL carry the `jobId`; `pass-succeeded` the primary `takeId`;
  `boundary-extracted` the artifact id and `sha256:` hash; every failure event a human-readable
  `reason`. These joins are what make take provenance answerable from disk after restart.

## 1.5 Creation and authorization (R-12..R-17)

- **R-12** Plan creation SHALL be atomic and idempotent, before any network I/O: compile the
  passes (#398), write the aggregate to a temp file, rename into place, then append
  `authorized`. The creating command carries a client `requestId` recorded in the aggregate; a
  redelivered command SHALL find the existing plan by `requestId` and return it rather than
  creating or spending anything twice. No pass may enqueue before the aggregate and its
  `authorized` event are durable.
- **R-13** Compilation refusals (stale frame selections, unsupported aspect, over-cap seconds,
  reference limits — #398) SHALL refuse plan creation with the compiler's own words. A plan
  that could not compile does not exist; there is nothing to recover.
- **R-14** Creation SHALL enqueue exactly the passes with no unmet dependencies (in a linear
  chain, pass 0). Each is `pass-materialised` then `pass-enqueued`, each event durable before
  the action it precedes.
- **R-15** The **aggregate cap** SHALL be the sum of every compiled pass's estimate at
  authorization. The cap is what the user confirmed; it is spelled in the confirmation copy and
  stored in micro-USD. Nothing materialised under this plan may push cumulative recorded
  estimates past the cap — over is a refusal, not a clamp.
- **R-16** The policy SHALL be one of two words. **`review-gated`**: after each pass succeeds,
  materialising the next requires a `continue-approved` event recorded from a visible user act;
  nothing continues by itself. **`pre-authorized`**: dependent passes materialise without
  another ask, up to the cap. There is no third policy and no per-pass override.
- **R-17** If a dependent pass's estimate at materialisation exceeds its authorized estimate —
  for any reason — the plan SHALL append `reconfirm-required` naming both figures and stop
  materialising until a `reconfirmed` event records a fresh visible act. Pre-authorization
  never covers money the user has not seen.

## 1.6 Materialisation (R-18..R-20)

- **R-18** A dependent pass SHALL materialise only when every dependency holds: the named
  pass `succeeded` (its primary take is on disk), and — for `boundary-frame` — the boundary
  still is extracted, filed as an image artifact with hash and `boundaryExtraction` provenance
  (issue #154's pipeline), and its `boundary-extracted` event is durable. Extraction failure
  appends `boundary-failed` with the source take named; the plan halts there as
  user-recoverable (retry extraction, or cancel) and SHALL NOT fall back to dispatching the
  pass frameless — a silently dropped frame is the failure this spec exists to prevent.
- **R-19** Materialisation SHALL bind the late frame fields into a copy of the compiled params
  (aggregate untouched), record the completed exact-sent bag in `pass-materialised`, and only
  then enqueue with the pass's pre-allocated idempotency key. Crash between the two: the fold
  sees `pass-materialised` without `pass-enqueued` and re-enqueues the **same** key — SPEC-009
  reconciliation makes the duplicate impossible from there.
- **R-20** Batch behaviour: independent passes MAY be enqueued in one batch, but each pass's
  events are appended individually and in order, so a batch that dies midway leaves every
  completed pass `pass-enqueued` and every incomplete one `pass-materialised` — the fold
  resumes each from its own truth. There is no cross-pass rollback: passes are separate spends,
  and un-spending a submitted job is not a thing SPEC-009 pretends to offer.

## 1.7 Outcomes, staleness, cancellation (R-21..R-26)

- **R-21** A pass is `succeeded` when its job succeeds AND its takes are finalized (SPEC-013).
  A job that succeeded whose finalization failed is `needs-reconciliation` — the fold surfaces
  it from the queue journal, and the pass neither succeeds nor unblocks dependents until the
  landing completes on a later open (SPEC-009 §2.5's recovery, unchanged).
- **R-22** A pass whose job terminally fails or is cancelled appends `pass-failed` with the
  queue's reason. Dependent passes never materialise past a failed dependency; the plan reaches
  `completed` with a partial outcome named per pass.
- **R-23** The plan is `completed` when no pass can change state: every pass terminal, or the
  chain halted by failure, staleness, refusal, or cancellation. `completed` is an event and a
  fold result, never an edit to the aggregate.
- **R-24** Staleness SHALL be checked at every materialisation against the aggregate's sources:
  scene version, cited sheet versions, canon revision, art direction version, production
  style override recorded in compiled params, production aspect, and the routed model. Any drift
  appends `stale` naming the moved source; running
  passes finish and land; nothing further materialises. Recovery is a new plan compiled from
  the world as it now stands.
- **R-25** `cancelled` stops all future materialisation immediately and requests cancellation
  of in-flight jobs through SPEC-009 (which owes no guarantee about jobs already running at a
  provider). Already-landed takes are unaffected — cancellation is about future spend, never
  about deleting paid work.
- **R-26** Take provenance linkage: a job created under a plan SHALL carry `planId` and
  `passIndex` in its params (stripped before the wire, recorded on the take like everything
  else in the bag), so any take answers "which authorization spent this" from its own record.

## 1.8 Source-of-truth map (R-27)

- **R-27** One owner per fact, and the fold joins them:

| Fact | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| What was authorized: passes, prompts, orders, cap, policy, sources | Plan aggregate (immutable) |
| What has happened to each pass, incl. late-bound frame fields | Plan events journal |
| Submission truth: submitted / remote id / retries / cost | SPEC-009 queue journal |
| Outcome media and frozen provenance | Takes (SPEC-013) |
| Boundary-frame bytes and extraction provenance | Artifact store (issue #154) |
| Money actually charged | Ledger (SPEC-008) |

Jobs never become a competing aggregate authority: a job knows its plan; the plan does not ask
jobs what was authorized.

# 2 · Design

## 2.1 Files

```
productions/<pid>/plans/
  pl_01J9….json           ← aggregate: written once, atomic rename, immutable
  pl_01J9….events.jsonl    ← journal: append-only, fsync before act
```

In the world folder deliberately: a plan is provenance the way takes are, travels with a world
export, and is scoped to the production it spends for. The queue journal stays app-root
(SPEC-009 R-2); the two meet only through `jobId`.

## 2.2 The aggregate, sketched

```jsonc
{
  "planId": "pl_01J9…",
  "requestId": "01J9…",              // creation idempotency (#384 pattern)
  "worldId": "01J8…", "productionId": "saltlight", "sceneId": "sc_04",
  "mode": "whole-scene",
  "policy": "review-gated",           // or "pre-authorized"
  "capMicroUsd": 812000,              // sum of pass estimates at authorization
  "sources": { "canonRevision": 42, "artDirectionVersion": 3, "sceneVersion": 2,
               "sheets": { "maren-kest": 4 }, "aspect": "9:16", "modelId": "wan-2.7" },
  "passes": [
    { "passIndex": 0, "idempotencyKey": "01J9…A", "dependsOn": [], /* CompiledPass … */ },
    { "passIndex": 1, "idempotencyKey": "01J9…B",
      "dependsOn": [{ "passIndex": 0, "needs": "boundary-frame" }],
      "route": { "kind": "frame", "mode": "first-frame", "endpoint": "fal-ai/wan/v2.7/image-to-video" },
      /* CompiledPass minus frame fields; bound at materialisation */ }
  ],
  "createdAt": "2026-08-20T12:00:00.000Z"
}
```

## 2.3 The state machine

Pass states, folded (never stored as a mutable field):

```
compiled ──materialised──▶ enqueued ──job succeeded + finalized──▶ succeeded
   │                          │
   │                          ├─ job failed/cancelled ────▶ failed
   │                          └─ job needs-reconciliation ─▶ waiting (SPEC-009 resolves)
   ├─ dependency failed / plan stale / cancelled ─────────▶ never-materialised (named)
   └─ boundary-failed ────────────────────────────────────▶ blocked (retry or cancel)
```

Plan states: `authorized → active → completed`, with `cancelled` and `stale` as halting marks
that still end in `completed` once running passes settle. Legal transitions are exactly the
event kinds of R-9; any other write is a bug, and the fold ignores rather than invents.

## 2.4 Recovery: every window, its truth, its recovery

| Crash window | On disk | Recovery (automatic unless marked) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before aggregate rename | nothing | Nothing existed; command retry recreates (same requestId) |
| Aggregate durable, no `authorized` | aggregate only | Append `authorized`, resume creation path (idempotent) |
| `pass-materialised`, no `pass-enqueued` | intent + key | Re-enqueue same idempotency key; SPEC-009 dedupes |
| `pass-enqueued`, job mid-flight | jobId join | Nothing to do — SPEC-009 owns submission recovery |
| Provider accepted, ack lost | job `submitting` | SPEC-009 reconcile (poll by idempotency key), then fold |
| Job succeeded, finalization failed | queue `needs-reconciliation` | SPEC-013 landing on next open; pass waits, dependents wait |
| Take landed, `pass-succeeded` unappended | take + job succeeded | Fold derives success from the join; append on next fold (idempotent) |
| Boundary extraction failed | `boundary-failed` | **User-required**: retry extraction or cancel plan |
| Extraction done, event unappended | artifact exists | Re-extraction is idempotent-by-content; append and continue |
| Source moved between passes | versions ≠ aggregate | Append `stale`; **user-required**: new plan |
| App crash / world switch / restart | all of the above | Open → fold; no timer, no screen, no memory |

## 2.5 Authorization math

The confirmation names one number: the cap (R-15). Review-gated plans re-present each dependent
pass's figure at its `continue-approved` moment, which is a repetition of recorded numbers, not
a recomputation. Pre-authorized plans spend recorded estimates only; the single legal deviation
is downward (a route offering fewer seconds than compiled is a compile-time refusal, not a
materialisation surprise). Anything upward is R-17's reconfirmation. Actual cost reconciliation
(estimates vs ledger) remains SPEC-008/009's, joined per pass through `jobId`.

## 2.6 Privacy, retention, compatibility

Plans contain prompts and file paths already present in jobs and takes; they add no new class
of data. They live in the world folder and therefore ride world export — which is wanted, since
they are the authorization half of provenance. Retention: kept indefinitely, like takes;
cancellation marks, never deletes. Compatibility: `plans/` is an unknown directory to older
builds and is ignored by their scanners; no world schema-version raise is required to hold
plans (SPEC-023's raise rules unchanged — writing a plan does not fence a world). The aggregate
carries no version field of its own; a future incompatible change mints `pl2_` rather than
mutating what `pl_` means.

# 3 · Decisions

- **D1 · Two files, one immutable.** Status inside the aggregate would mean editing the
  authorization record on every event; an events-only design would recompute authorization from
  a replay. Immutable aggregate + append journal gives each need its natural shape and makes
  "what did the user approve" a byte-stable question.
- **D2 · Idempotency keys minted at creation, not at enqueue.** SPEC-009's exactly-once
  guarantee keys on the idempotency key existing durably before submission. Minting at plan
  creation puts every future submission's key inside the immutable aggregate — the
  materialisation crash window (R-19) then has nothing to invent.
- **D3 · The plan holds CompiledPass verbatim.** Any summarised copy would be a second dispatch
  authority. Issue #398 built the one object; this spec stores it and adds only what dispatch
  cannot know yet (late frame fields) or must not recompute (keys, DAG).
- **D4 · Frames block, never degrade.** A pass that declared a boundary-frame dependency
  refuses to run without it (R-18). The alternative — dispatch frameless and hope — spends
  money on the exact discontinuity the dependency was declared to prevent.
- **D5 · Staleness halts forward motion only.** Killing running jobs on source drift would
  destroy paid work to enforce a consistency the user can evaluate themselves; materialising
  new passes against a moved world would spend against sources nobody reviewed. Finish what
  runs, refuse what follows, name what moved.
- **D6 · Two policies, no ladder.** Per-pass authorization matrices invite states nobody can
  audit. Review-gated and pre-authorized cover the two honest intents — "ask me each time" and
  "I approved this much" — and R-17 keeps the second honest under drift.
- **D7 · The fold joins three journals rather than mirroring them.** Copying queue status into
  plan events would create the competing authority R-27 forbids; folding at read keeps each
  journal's writer single and the join pure.
- **D8 · World-resident storage.** A plan in the app root would divorce authorization from the
  world it spent for: exports would lose provenance, and two worlds could not be audited
  independently. The queue stays global; the plan stays with the work.

# 4 · Adversarial test matrix (for #402)

Each T names the invariant it attacks. Implementation lands with this matrix as tests.

- **T-1** Create twice with one requestId → one plan, one spend path, second call returns the first.
- **T-2** Kill between aggregate rename and `authorized` → reopen resumes, no duplicate pass 0.
- **T-3** Kill between `pass-materialised` and enqueue → re-enqueue reuses the recorded key; the queue holds one job.
- **T-4** Batch of two independent passes dies after the first enqueue → fold resumes the second only.
- **T-5** Pass 0 succeeds; extraction fails → `boundary-failed` names the take; pass 1 never enqueues; retry extraction unblocks.
- **T-6** Extraction succeeds; crash before `boundary-extracted` → reopen re-extracts idempotently and continues.
- **T-7** Review-gated: pass 1 ready, no `continue-approved` → nothing materialises across restarts, forever.
- **T-8** Pre-authorized: estimates sum to the cap → last pass runs; one micro-USD over → refusal, `reconfirm-required`.
- **T-9** Materialisation estimate above authorized figure → halt with both numbers; `reconfirmed` resumes.
- **T-10** Scene version bumps mid-plan → `stale` names the scene; running pass lands; pass 2 never materialises.
- **T-11** Cancel with one job in flight → no new materialisation; SPEC-009 cancel requested; landed takes untouched.
- **T-12** Job succeeds, finalization fails → pass neither succeeds nor unblocks; SPEC-013 landing completes it on next open.
- **T-13** Provider accepted, ack lost → SPEC-009 reconcile; fold reflects the found submission without a second job.
- **T-14** Events replayed twice / delivered out of order within a line-append's guarantees → identical fold.
- **T-15** Unknown event kind in the journal → preserved, ignored, fold unaffected.
- **T-16** Take from a plan job → params record `planId`/`passIndex`; wire bag carries neither.
- **T-17** Frame-dependent pass compiled → priced on the frame route's lengths at authorization, not the text route's.
- **T-18** A video take id in a frame slot → refused at compile (issue #154's guards), plan never created.
- **T-19** World closed and reopened mid-plan → fold from disk equals the pre-close state exactly.
- **T-20** Older build opens a world with plans → ignores `plans/`, world loads, nothing lost on next new-build open.
