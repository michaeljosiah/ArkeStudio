Arke can now write on your own machine. With no cloud key stored, its agents run on a local model
through Ollama, with a context window of at least 128k and up to 256k. Between sessions it keeps a
checklist, notes on each world and a page about you. Long conversations are compacted around what
was decided, what is still open and what you asked it to keep. A model that has to be chosen by
name, such as Gemma 4 12B Uncensored, waits until you choose it, and Arke unloads what it loaded
when it quits.

A world can be founded in conversation: say what it is, and the founding chat drafts it with you
before the build makes it.

The audiobook takes direction. Markers set a line's delivery, a pause or a cue. A Performed
reading lets the narrator play each speaker. A narrator can be chosen for the whole book, and the
dialog shows what the switch costs before you press. A speaker a person records goes out as a
script and comes back as takes. With a Google key, Gemini's preset voices join the readers.

Studio opens on your phone. Pair it over Tailscale from Settings, Remote access, with a code or a
link. The launch, Home, the world, its Bible, canon, World Chat, artifacts and productions are laid
out for a phone and for an unfolded Fold7.

Interactive productions gain a branch map and a player, and a second kind: the visual novel.
Pictures, lines and voices are read at your own pace, with a choice at the end of each scene, and
exported as a web package.

Video editions can be published with captions and played on their own, delivered as a folder or a
ZIP. A world and a production can each choose their own models, inheriting Settings until they do.
