title: Pictures on the words, Tall pictures and music
date: 2026-10-25
picture: picture.jpg
---
A chapter's pictures can now begin at any sentence, not only at the start of a block. Illustrate
reads the story sentence by sentence and proposes where each picture belongs, and you can add a
picture at a sentence or drag one along the words. Every picture can also be made Tall, a 9:16
portrait for phones, from the Wide one or alongside it, and an export can choose a smaller size.

Music is now a kind of production. Make an album, add tracks with their composer and takes, edit a
track and set the album's sequence. A world's Guide can carry a theme and a song teaser, and World
Chat can reach the music.

Arke Player editions can release new chapters as a part, and a world's teasers travel with its
Guide. A chapter can have its own narrator, credited in the header and the cast. Large worlds stay
connected through long picture runs, and local ComfyUI models now decide for themselves whether
there is memory to run.

Worlds saved by this release cannot be opened by earlier releases.
