A chapter now opens as a writing room: the page across the window, one bar above it with the
chapter's goal, Focus and Compare, and the names of the world's characters and places linked in
the prose. Hover a name to see who it is, type @ to put one in, and / for Arke's own commands:
Continue, Describe and Dialogue. Select a passage and Ask Arke to tighten it, expand it or change
its tone; the change waits in the page as struck and marked words until you keep or refuse it.
A finished review puts its notes in the margin beside the words they quote.

Each chapter also has a Chapter view: its picture, the book as a strip of chapters, the cast as
cards, and how far it is written, cast, read and mixed. Audio Studio arranges a read chapter's
voices, beds and sounds on a timeline, and the Cut gains crossfades, air particles and effects
that follow the sound.

A story can be published privately as an EPUB to read, or as an M4B narrated with the voices it
was cast with. Chat follows you through the app and knows what page you are on.

Large worlds open several times faster: a world with thousands of pictures and takes no longer
reads itself in full five times before it shows.
