Everything past the world's front door now fits a phone and an unfolded Fold7: the cast and a
character's pages, art direction, scenes and shots, Generate and the frame run, the Cut, Develop and
the overview, seasons and the branch map, chapters and the audiobook, and Settings. On a phone each
page is one column, with sheets from the foot where the desktop has a side panel. On the Fold7 the
production's rail keeps its words beside the page.

The Cut takes touch. Pick a clip and the tool bar becomes that clip's: split, trim, duplicate,
delete. Trims are typed times rather than dragged handles. In a chapter, a selected passage keeps
the phone's own menu, and the ask sits in a bar at the foot.

A paired phone sees its own conversations and its own device. Keys, pairing and revoking stay on
the PC.

With a Google key stored, a character's voice can be designed from the words on their sheet. Each
candidate is priced before it is made, and the voice is saved with the world for its sheet and the
audiobook to use.
