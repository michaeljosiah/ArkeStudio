The creation door asks two questions instead of showing a form: what are you making, then what
kind. Arke is a panel docked on the page beside the thing it is making, at every level, season,
episode, scene, shot, and the screen shows what the conversation is making while it makes it.

The proposal machinery came off the screen. What a person sees is Arke's sentence, the things it
made, and two buttons: Apply changes or Keep discussing. A diff appears only where something
would be overwritten, and it groups by the thing it changes.

The generation plan stopped being a page. Review scene is a strip above the shots, in the scene's
own words; Generate scene is a drawer with one model, one estimate, and Generation options behind
its own door. Generate is a visual workspace where takes sit side by side and Arke takes notes
beside them.

The rule underneath all four turns: every level has three layers, the thing, Arke, and Advanced,
and layer three never visually dominates layer one.

Fifteen verified findings from a review of the branch, all fixed. Two were on the direct
scene-write path: a frame that accepted an unconstrained string joined into the world directory,
and a write that merged rather than replaced, so a deleted field came back from the old document
while the version bumped.

A ten-turn season conversation inside a real world, pushing back on the first shape, keeping two
ideas out of seven, contradicting canon, holding two spines side by side, recalling a decision
from six turns earlier, and reversing the ending after settling it. The wrap-up wrote the season
the conversation actually ended on.
