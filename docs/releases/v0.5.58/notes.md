title: More room for the canvas
date: 2026-10-07
picture: picture.jpg
---
On a Fold or a tablet, an open Studio now gives its canvas the width. The production's rail folds
to its marks while Studio is open, the conversation keeps 380 pixels, and the canvas takes the
rest: about half again as wide as before in portrait, and wider still in landscape. Close Studio
and the full rail comes back.

The canvas head is two lines instead of a stack of buttons: the production with Pin on one, and
its views as one row of tabs on the other. Back to card shows only when a card opened the canvas,
and the Conversation and Canvas switch is kept for phones, where the two take turns.
