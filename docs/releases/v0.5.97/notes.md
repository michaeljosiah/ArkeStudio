title: Graphic novels, stories on your phone and a simpler studio
date: 2026-11-04
picture: picture.jpg

Graphic novels are now a kind of production. Pick a comic style and Arke plans a chapter, drafts its
pages and reads them cold, as someone with only the pages would. Each page is made in one picture,
and its lettering is read back and checked. A finished book exports as PDF, CBZ or EPUB, or goes to
Arke Player.

Interactive movies and visual novels now reach Arke Player as packages of their own, and a story
plays itself, stopping only where there is a choice to make. Audiobooks carry opening and closing
credits in every format, and can export mastered chapter MP3s. Any picture can have a Tall version
chosen from the world's own art, without making a new one.

Review manuscript reads a whole book and writes an editor's report, which you can save as a PDF.
The Studio assistant helps with setup and local AI, System shows what your machine is doing, and
Diagnostics names a fault and what to do next. Chat takes your next message while Arke is still
working, and Pika joins the local video models.

The studio is simpler throughout. A new world names itself from your first idea and begins in one
press. Dashboards lead with what to do next, Activity gathers its rows, empty screens show pictures,
and internal words and ids have given way to plain ones. Chapters left untitled go out by their
number.

Worlds saved by this release cannot be opened by earlier releases.
