The narrator voice picker now searches your complete voice catalogue, including large cloud
libraries. Filter by provider, language, accent, gender and style where those details are available.
Your pending choice stays selected while you narrow the results.

Hear a voice before choosing it. Play a provider sample or generate a preview with its price shown
before you press Play. Local previews are free, and saved previews can be replayed from the cache.
Playing a sample does not change your narrator; Use saves your choice.

Remote access now chooses an available address automatically, making it easier to connect another
device to your studio.
