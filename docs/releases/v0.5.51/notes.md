Qwen Image 2.1 is a new local image recipe, downloaded like the others and run on your own
graphics card: square pictures at 1024, and one optional reference to edit from. It is marked
Research because that is what its licence allows. Qwen's terms permit noncommercial research and
evaluation only, so pictures made with it are not for a production you mean to sell.

It asks a lot of the machine: a 10 GB card and 30 GB of memory, with room free on both when it
starts. Where the machine falls short, the recipe says so and stays off. On the managed engine it
starts its own worker when the download finishes, with the careful settings it needs, and leaves
Krea and the other recipes running as they were. An engine you run yourself at your own address
needs that setup done by hand.

The managed ComfyUI engine moves to 0.37.0 for every local recipe, checked against the digest its
publishers put on the download.
