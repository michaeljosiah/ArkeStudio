---
title: Comics panel by panel, and cards that say what they do
date: 2026-11-07
picture: picture.jpg
---
Graphic novels gain a second way to make a page. Panel by panel draws each panel on its own and
places the words itself, so one panel can be redrawn without touching the rest. Arke can now make a
whole chapter from one priced card: it draws the panels, redraws what it finds at fault, places the
lines, and leaves you the panels that need a look and the pages to accept. A new book can start
without a name, and keeping a plan starts the draft.

Arke no longer tells you it has no world tools. It was telling the truth: a turn could reach the
model before its tools had connected, and now it waits for them. Opening a production's conversation
from World Chat no longer crashes the screen. A picture made in World Chat now arrives on its card,
and can be used as the world's key art or master look from there.

Arke's cards say what they do. A picture's card shows the prompt, the model and the price, with the
rest one press away. A chapter plan reads as its chapters. A chapter's draft is read on its page,
and a planned chapter offers Draft with Arke where you are looking at it.

The studio is simpler again. A production with nothing made shows one next step, and its model
choices fold into one line. A book's Artifacts opens on its own files, and its pictures stay out of
the world's. Activity gathers what failed the same way, and notices stop stacking over the screen
that already shows the result. The window opens in your theme.

Chapters save when the window is hidden, and the first edit of written prose keeps the original.
Audiobooks list the quotations with no speaker, for you to give a voice or keep as narration.

Worlds saved by this release cannot be opened by earlier releases.
