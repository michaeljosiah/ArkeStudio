The Undersong returns with Perrin's awakening: a revised 23-chapter novel, 290 illustrations,
world Bible, cast, looks and locations. Read the illustrated EPUB, listen to all 23 chapters
in the single-narrator M4B, or follow the pictures and narration in the portable player.
Whole-book listening qualification remains pending. The video pilot is unfinished and video
production is paused.

Choose Settings, then Sample world, to download the current editable story. The 5.16 GB
package includes the current reading and listening editions along with retained artwork,
takes and authored history. Arke downloads and verifies every part before installing a new
world; existing worlds stay intact. The lightweight test fixture remains separate.

Chapter actions now stay within reach while reading on a phone or tablet. Close a block's
inspector without losing the selected passage, return to its chosen tab, and move through
Looks, Direct or Export while keeping your place. Phone Looks sheets retain the chapter's
context, and nested drafts and keyboard focus return to the control you came from.
