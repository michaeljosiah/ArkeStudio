The world door produced a cast, some places and a few open questions and threw away the
reasoning that produced all three. A world is now born with its bible: the through-line the
founding conversation wrote, in the author's words, at v1 and editable on arrival. World Chat
can start one for a world that has none, and says so rather than waiting to be asked.

Worlds can be renamed. The name is a label and the folder underneath never moves, so a world
called after the wrong thing on day one is not called that forever.

Arke can read a page online when you let it. Off until you turn it on, under Harness, and every
page it reads is kept as an attachment you can quote.

Seasons can be as long as the form actually runs: eight to a hundred episodes rather than five
to twelve, because a vertical series is sixty to a hundred drops and a season written to eight
has a different spine from one written to eighty. Seedance 2.5 arrives beside 2.0, thirty
seconds in one call against fifteen, with its own authoring guidance.

The camera a director sets now reaches the model. Size, angle, lens, focus, movement, pace,
light, time of day and grade were written to disk and dropped before the prompt was built; so
were the things a shot said to keep out of frame, and its ambience and effects. All of them
travel now.

And a decision nobody could make stops being offered: a change the world already contains
retires itself instead of sitting in Needs you, unacceptable and unclearable, forever.

Behind those: the Cut takes any artifact on any lane and finally makes a sound that reaches the
exported file; the world lock is held and released by its owner; ownership is a revision; and
the character sheet has its own layout back after a shot drawer quietly took its class names.
