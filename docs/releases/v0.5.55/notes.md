date: 2026-09-29

---
Add your remote Studio to your phone's home screen with the Arke icon and open it in its own
window. On iPhone, use Share then Add to Home Screen. On Android, use Install app or Add to Home
screen in your browser menu. Remote access settings include these instructions. An installed app
that asks to pair needs approval on your PC once more.

Stalled connection checks now time out and retry, and returning to Studio or restoring your
network starts another check. Older iPhones can use the same connection recovery. Pairing
submissions keep waiting for their one-use response, and browser storage restrictions get their
own explanation.

Your PC still holds your worlds and runs Studio. Keep it awake with Studio and Tailscale running;
home-screen installation does not add offline editing. If an existing shortcut has no icon,
remove it and add Studio to your home screen again after updating.

This release also includes the Gemini voice catalogue fixes and the updated text-to-speech
choices in Settings, with IndexTTS removed from the available engines.
