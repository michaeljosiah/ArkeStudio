The audiobook has its own page now: the cover as a backdrop, the cast as portraits, the chapters
listed with how far each is read, and Continue to pick up where you stopped. The player shows the
chapter's picture of the moment and the words as they are read, with speed and a sleep timer.
Export adds a video, one file a chapter or one for the book, with captions, at 16:9 or vertical.

Each chapter can be directed and illustrated. Arke reads the book and the chapter notes and
proposes how each block is delivered. It proposes pictures for the chapter, made one at a time
once you accept them. Each character has looks: what they wear in this chapter, made into a
full-body picture that the chapter's pictures follow. A Timing view lays the voices out in lanes,
with pauses, overlaps, reactions, beds and sounds.

Reading is quicker and steadier. Gemini reads a run of blocks in one request and the app cuts it
at the reader's own pauses. One direction carries across the Bench, the audiobook and a scene's
lines. Blocks can be joined and split, and an edited line keeps its speaker. A provider's free
plan reads voices without a confirmation until its limit, and every chat can read Arke's replies
aloud.

Choosing a model is one chip in the composer: search, the providers in groups, a card for each
model with its price, and the effort beside it. Production Chat can now prepare and run work
across a production: frames, takes, dialogue, sound and the whole cut, with each decision kept in
the conversation. On the Bench, H3 Video takes up to nine reference pictures, a take can be upscaled
to 1080p on your own machine, and local sampling has presets.
