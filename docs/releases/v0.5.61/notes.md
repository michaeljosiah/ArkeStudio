The Undersong now downloads when you choose it. Open Settings, then Sample world, to see
the release size and what is finished before downloading your own editable copy. Installing
again creates another world; your existing work stays intact.

This edition contains The Ledger of Nights, a sixteen-chapter novel with 244 illustrations,
world and character references, an EPUB, and six illustrated single-narrator audiobook
chapter players. The audiobook is unfinished: ten chapters still lack completed exports.
The video pilot is unfinished too: its plan and stills are included, but there is no finished
video episode. Final editorial and world-reference review remain in progress.

The full story is a separate 1.60 GB download. Its integrity is checked before installation,
and failed downloads can be retried. The small world used by automated tests is kept separate
and no longer adds content to the installer.
