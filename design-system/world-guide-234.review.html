Maren Kest
Public
ShowsFrom the startAfter a chapter
Sections
Essence

Tide-caller. She hears the verse under the harbour and pulls the water where it needs to go. She has paid for it: her left ear is done, and she knows the right is next.

Appearance

Salt-crusted braids, pale grey eyes, oilskin coat with a bioluminescent thread at the collar.

RelationshipsAfter Chapter 6

Trusts Bray with her life, not her secrets. The Chorister knows her true note, and Maren does not know how.

Voice · written

Low and even. Speaks to the water before she speaks to people. Never wastes a word she could keep.
