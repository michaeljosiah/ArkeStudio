How a season becomes a film

One pattern, three times: a chat that makes it, and a screen that shows what it made. The season decides what the thing is; an episode decides what happens in it and makes the scenes it needs; a scene decides how it is shot. Nothing below a level exists until the level above has said what it needs, which is why the arrows only point one way. Beside every chat is what it understood so far; beside every details screen is what is staged and waiting on a yes — two different things, never drawn as one.

  • The details moved out. What the conversation settles is read next door, on Season or Overview, which is its own rail item. Every screen used to be half a place to make something and half a place to read it, and a person could not tell which they were doing.
