# Turn 253 · The page review

Proposed 1 November 2026. Approved by the owner the same day ("Approved"), calls 1 – 5 as drawn. SPEC-064 revision 8, R-67 – R-73 (specs PR 124).

## Why this turn

The owner read The Ledger of Nights, drawn, as published and asked why the pictures had been checked only for their words. The lettering check reads text and nothing else. On that book it:

- passed a caption about stopping a pen, drawn over Maren at the rail;
- passed faces in the water that no panel asked for, and Bray without his collar, both caught only by eye;
- failed the ledger's own handwriting as extra (issue 2266), which had to be accepted anyway.

The spec's layout and continuity checks (R-17, R-21) were never built. Revision 8 adds one review of the picture for all of it.

## What it reuses

- **Turn 251's Pages** (251a – 251d), in its own markup and sizing rule.
- **Turn 247's findings**: boxes on the page, rows under the verdict.
- **The take record**: verification, and the `acknowledged` list that has had no producer.
- **Settings › Harness**: a model per agent; the page reviewer is a new agent beside the lettering reader.

## The frames

| Frame | What it decides |

## Calls for the owner

1. Two verdict lines, Lettering and Picture.
2. Picture faults boxed and listed like lettering ones, and blocking.
3. Cleared writing stays listed, struck through.
4. Warnings take Seen and do not hold Accept.
5. Not checked names the reason and offers Choose reviewer and Check again.

## Not drawn

The script reader's findings on a draft (R-64 – R-66), the page plan (R-60 – R-63) and Book read in readiness (R-74). Each gets its own turn. Checking is 251c's band, unchanged.
