# Turn 254 · Adapt before drawing

Proposed 1 November 2026. SPEC-064 revision 8, R-60 – R-66 (specs PR 124).

## Why this turn

The owner read The Ledger of Nights, drawn, and found parts that made no sense and did not flow. Arke had read all 23 chapters, then drafted straight from the prose. It measured itself by fidelity and wrote for someone who knew the novel:

- people spoken of before they were shown;
- a chain, a lamp, bell pins and a warning nothing on the pages set up;
- seven pages for a chapter whose cuts left later lines leaning on what was cut.

Nothing read the draft as a reader would. The owner asked for the page plan and the cold read in one turn.

## What it reuses

- **Pages** as turn 249 drew it and as it ships: the empty chapter (249c), the draft with its bar, Keep and Discard (249d).
- **SPEC-052's adaptation record**: passages judged Keep, Cut, Merge, Show or Invent, each with a reason.
- **Settings › Harness**: a model per agent; the script reader is a new agent.

## Calls for the owner

1. Plan with Arke comes before Draft with Arke.
2. The plan is passages, pages and what the reader knows.
3. A line that leans on a cut is named in the plan.
4. Every draft is read cold; what is left is marked on the page with the reader's question.
5. Blocking findings turn Keep into Keep anyway.
