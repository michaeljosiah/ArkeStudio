Binding proposal

1 · Current design sources. Use the existing 227a/227m/227k master pages, 206b notification drawing, global-chat-224.composer.mjs and its native ModelChip specimen, and icons rendered from packages/client/src/components/icons.tsx. The composer send glyph is rendered from composer.tsx. The Arke welcome mark remains the existing lowercase a. Do not introduce replacement chrome, sidebar, account badge, provider pills or icon paths. Reference snapshots record their sources and icon hash.

2 · Review status. 26 October 2026 · Revision 2 · Proposed for author review, not approved or built. The owner requested a global-chat design turn using the notification dialog’s rounded window. Frames reuse the current 227 writing-room drawings, the actual 206b Activity reference, the approved 225 shared composer and generated native ModelChip, and SVGs rendered from current client icon components. Operational values are illustrative.

3 · Studio scope. This dialog is the application-level Arke assistant: help, setup, downloads, local AI and operational attention. It has the visible Studio scope even with a world open. This extends turn 225’s first-increment restriction before opening a world; it does not give the Studio conversation implicit world/production tools. World Chat and production build chat retain their own layouts, transcripts, drafts, cards and captured authority.

4 · One shell grammar. Use turn 206 Activity’s desktop shell: 12px corner radius on all four corners, full 1px border, card surface, existing large shadow, top 50px, right 10px and bottom 12px from the viewport, under the titlebar. Desktop starts at 440px wide. Float over the page rather than reserve a right column or squeeze its contents. The Activity reference is drawn beside this proposal to make the relationship reviewable.

5 · Identity and entry. Keep the existing Chat entry in the titlebar, with its quiet attention state. Opening this entry opens the Studio assistant. Header: Arke, Studio assistant, History, Close. A persistent scope row says Studio / Setup, local AI & help. Page navigation does not retarget this conversation. Existing contextual entry points continue to open their own World/Builder chat. This new routing requires implementation; it is not a restatement of current turn 225 behavior.

6 · Conversation and composer. Keep the shared composer, attachment/dictation lifecycle, supported model/effort controls, unsent draft and current conversation identity. The model chip names the configured model and effort appears only when supported. The compact specimen draws the controls without recreating the picker. Ordinary attachment, model and microphone behavior still needs native implementation/parity checks. No typing, dictation, notification or navigation auto-sends or approves.

7 · Measured diagnosis. Arke remains the visible speaker. Operational investigation may use the dedicated diagnostic capability behind it; the product states Checking storage rather than implementation details. Explain the measured cause, affected work and next action. A storage card shows destination label, freshness, available bytes, known peak plus reserve and deficit. Stale/unknown evidence stays explicitly stale/unknown. The card never claims that service health proves a finished export.

8 · Remedies and approval. Review cleanup opens an exact plan and performs no deletion. A gated card names the action, estimated saving, files retained and consequences, with separate Clear previews and Keep files controls. Execution must revalidate scope, ownership, leases and evidence under SPEC-061/062. State recovered only after actual verification. The fixture’s success is illustrative and has no host effects. Creative artifacts, takes, recordings, exports and history are retained.

9 · Explicit world and production handoff. A creative request offers Open World Chat or the matching production conversation, naming the target. On a confirmed handoff carry only the explicit request as an editable draft; preserve the global draft/history. Do not copy diagnostic logs, silently merge transcripts, submit the request or infer permission from navigation. Production and world chat are task-specific workspaces, not restyled into this dialog by this turn.

10 · Proactive attention. A failed event or advance storage warning appears in existing Activity/Needs you and the quiet Chat attention state. It never opens the dialog or interrupts typing. Opening a notice resumes the matching incident conversation/card. The background recovery service still detects and protects without a running chat model; Arke presents results when opened. No separate diagnostic-agent conversation is required for a normal incident.

11 · Desktop and tablet interaction. Desktop is a named nonmodal dialog; workspace interaction remains possible and opening focuses the composer. Tablet under 1100px uses a modal overlay, at most 520px wide with 16px outer gaps; the background is inert and focus is contained. Reuse overlay coordination: Activity/Settings temporarily cover the retained conversation, without mounting another transcript or running two decision surfaces.

12 · Phone and keyboard. Below 600px use a modal rounded dialog, inset 12px from the sides and bottom safe area, below the persistent 48px titlebar plus 12px gap. Keep all four corners rounded, independent transcript scrolling, a stable header and the shared composer above the visual keyboard viewport. Apply 44px minimum touch targets. Small visible heights scroll messages rather than push the send or close control offscreen. Physical-device keyboard behavior remains a separate acceptance check.

13 · Close and focus. Close or Escape dismisses the dialog and restores its entry point without cancelling work or discarding a draft. History stays inside the Studio scope and does not combine world transcripts. Background clicks do not discard content. Modal focus containment, focus return, safe-area behavior and overlay suspension must be implemented using the existing dialog/portal owners.

14 · Acceptance. Verify desktop, tablet, 390px phone and reduced keyboard viewport in light and dark: 12px shell corners, full border, no clipped controls or horizontal overflow, independent scrolling, 44px touch controls, explicit scope, draft retention on close, diagnostic-plan approval separation and no automatic send during handoff. Illustrative prototype interactions and measured layout checks do not establish native application behavior.
