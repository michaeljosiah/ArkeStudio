# Turn 256 · Arke drives, the screen stays simple

Proposed 1 November 2026. Approved by the owner the same day ("Proceed to build"), calls 1 – 5 as drawn. SPEC-064, after revision 8.

## Why this turn

The owner asked for a run making a graphic novel entirely in Arke, with Claude acting as the person, and to look out for where the screens could be simpler: "most humans will want the AI to drive complex features and will want a simplified interface that still looks capable." The run made The Ledger of Nights, redrawn, chapter 1, in installed 0.5.77 – 0.5.79. What it met:

- Setup asked reading and page shape that were already chosen, in long replies, and showed a Models panel with a video model a comic never uses.
- Arke said it would pilot chapter 2; the plan and Start the pilot used chapter 1.
- Start the pilot had already asked Arke for the plan, but Pages still offered Plan with Arke; pressing it filled the chat box with a duplicate.
- Plan with Arke and Draft with Arke only filled the chat box; a second press sent them.
- Every draft was decided twice over: Keep in Pages and Approve on the chat's card.
- Fix on a lean could not work while the plan waited: Arke refuses a second draft of a chapter.
- The cold read counted the book's intended mysteries as faults: 19 to fix, most of them on purpose.
- Every page was given the same five-panel template, one of them holding nine panels.
- Three replies failed as "not in the shape the studio needs" before a person could do anything.
- The Dashboard was the writing one: words today, Continue chapter.
- The draft opened on ¶ citations, Shows and lettering kinds before the words.
