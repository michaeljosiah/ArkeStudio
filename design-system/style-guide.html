Arke Worlds · design system

Style guide

How Arke writes and how Arke looks. This page is the reference every document, screen and page in this folder is expected to match. It records rules that were already being followed — it does not invent new ones.

Every specimen below is drawn with live tokens. If a swatch is wrong, the token is wrong.
Scope

Writing rules and visual rules. Component anatomy, the token reference and the ~90 Binding decisions in Arke Studio.dc.html are not restated here — that document remains the record for what a screen does. This one governs how anything new is worded and styled.

01The two names

Arke Worlds is the parent brand. Arke Studio is the product inside it. The distinction is not decorative — it decides which name goes on a thing, and getting it wrong makes the company look like it has two identities.

RuleWhy
The YouTube channel, the wordmark on the banner and anything audience-facing about the company is Arke Worlds.The channel is a devblog about building a product; the publisher is the company, not the product.
The application, its screens, its releases, its licence line and the marketing site are Arke Studio.That is the thing being downloaded. Arke Studio · clickable flow, github.com/michaeljosiah/ArkeStudio.
Never write Arke alone as the product name in prose.It reads as a third brand. Arke alone is only acceptable inside the app's own voice, where context is unambiguous — "Arke is starting", "Arke shows the ripple then waits".
The wordmark is live text, never an asset.Two spans and a font. Dark mode needs no second file, and the mark re-themes from tokens like everything else. Stated in loading.html.
The thesis line is “The world is the asset.”It appears as the banner tagline, in the channel description and in the site footer. It is one string. Extended forms — Author once. Produce everywhere. — follow it; they do not replace it.

02Voice

Arke writes in short declaratives that end in a full stop. The characteristic sentence is four to seven words, states a constraint, and does not soften it. Claims are made plainly and then qualified in the sentence after, never hedged inside the claim itself.

Headlines, verbatim from the site and waitlist

Nothing changes until you accept.

Canon that knows when to refuse.

Identity that travels.

Your world lives with you.

Sentence case · full stop · no colon-subtitle · no exclamation.

RuleWhy
Say what the thing does, not how it feels."Built to remember what other tools forget" survives a sceptical reader. "Revolutionary AI-powered worldbuilding" does not.
The reader is “you”. The system is named, never “we”.Second person for the creator; the product acts in the third person — "Arke shows the ripple then waits", "the screen is the gate". We appears only in the devblog voice, where a person really is speaking.
State the ratio out loud."Mostly this is the devblog" costs nine words and keeps the audience that stays. Same instinct as "Arke Studio is early" — a reader forgives a rough edge they were warned about and resents one they discover.
Name what is wrong."What we get wrong" is the whole difference between a devblog and a marketing feed. The design documents already do this — they say plainly what is designed and not yet written.
No hype adjectives, no exclamation marks, no emoji.Status is carried by dots and pills, not faces. There is no emoji anywhere in the current asset set and there should not start being one.
Parallel structure is a signature; use it deliberately."Author once, produce everywhere." "Voice proposes. The screen disposes." "Local first · Open source · Human accepted · Provider independent." Two or three beats, never four for the sake of it.
AI is a skill the product has, not an identity it claims."How to direct image and video models" sits inside a list of craft. The channel is found by novelists and directors, and is not branded as an AI channel — that audience churns and is not the one being courted.
Write

Nothing changes until you accept.

The canon doesn't answer this, and it won't guess.

Arke Studio is early, free and open source.

Not

You're always in control of your creative vision!

Sorry, I couldn't find an answer to that question.

Introducing the future of AI-powered storytelling.

03Casing, spelling, punctuation

RuleWhy
Sentence case everywhere — headings, buttons, labels, menu items, table headers in prose.Title Case reads as a marketing brochure and forces a judgement call on every preposition.
Small labels may be uppercase, in mono, at .14em tracking.The eyebrow is the house label form: THE UNDERSONG · WORLD LOOK v3, HOLD TO SPEAK, FREE · MIT LICENCE. It is the one place uppercase is allowed.
British English.licence, organised, realisation, behaviour, colour, canonisation. Already consistent across the site, the waitlist and the requirements spec — the exception is color inside CSS, which is a keyword, not a word.
The middle dot · is the separator.Not a pipe, not an en dash, not a bullet. Local first · Open source · Provider independent. One space either side.
Em dashes set off a clause; they do not join a list.Spaced or unspaced consistently within a document, never mixed.
Curly quotes in prose, straight quotes in code.Anything in var(--font-mono) is a literal and is reproduced exactly.
Numerals from zero. Prices are exact or tilde'd, never both.$0.03 is a price. ~$0.61 is an estimate. A per-unit price carries no tilde — the binding rule already in the master document.

04Microcopy

Four forms, and each one has a fixed shape. Mixing them is what makes an interface feel written by several people.

FormShapeExample
ButtonVerb first, sentence case, no full stopGenerate specification · Accept · Open as thread · Hold to speak

Empty stateStates the answer, never blankwhatever OpenCode is set to
RuleWhy
A screen labels; it does not explain.Binding, from the master document. Explanation belongs in the docs, in a callout, or nowhere.
Every ending is named.A silent nothing reads as a failure. Success, refusal, empty and error each get words.
Empty is an answer.An unset field says what it is inheriting rather than showing blank. Blank looks broken.
A confirm names the outcome, it does not ask “are you sure?”."Move this world to archive" tells the reader what will happen. "Are you sure?" tells them nothing and trains them to click through.
What cannot travel is shown with its reason, never hidden.An unusable model stays listed, unselectable, sorted below, saying what is missing — "needs a FAL key". Hiding it makes the product look broken instead of unconfigured.
Consent is a checkbox, not a paragraph.One literal sentence the person can read in full: The person speaking agreed to have their voice cloned.
No figure the product cannot stand behind.Never invent a cost estimate to fill a slot. Where a number is not knowable, offer stoppability instead.

05How a design decision is written

Every design decision in this folder is recorded in the same three parts, and new documents follow the same form. It is the reason the master document is usable as a record rather than a gallery.

Binding. A screen labels; it does not explain.

The panel names the field and its value and stops. Anything that needs a sentence of justification is either a callout the reader can dismiss or it does not belong on the screen at all.

Binding · aphorism · reason. Nothing else.

RuleWhy
Open with Binding.It marks a constraint the implementation is expected to match, not a suggestion. If it is a suggestion, it is prose, not a rule.
The rule itself is one aphorism, bolded, sentence case, ending in a full stop.A rule that takes a paragraph to state cannot be checked against a screen.
The reason follows in one short paragraph.Six months later the reason is the part that is load-bearing — it tells the next reader whether the rule still applies.
Supersession is recorded, never silent.supersedes 1c, amends 41a, extends 65a. A rule that quietly replaces another leaves two live contradictions in the record.
Disagreements with an earlier document are stated and reasoned.since-the-prototype.html is the model: the prototype stays the reference for what it covers, the delta page says what changed and why.
Every review page closes with “what is not in this page”.An unstated gap reads as an oversight. A stated one reads as scope.

06Colour

The chrome is monochrome so the creator's imagery carries all the colour. This is the single most consequential visual rule in the system and everything else follows from it. White or near-black surfaces, hairline neutral borders, one red reserved for destructive and blocked, and colour that appears only in small state dots — never as a fill, never as chrome.

RuleWhy
Chrome stays neutral monochrome. The creator's imagery carries all the colour.The product's whole pitch is that the world is the asset. A branded interface competes with the thing the user made.
Hairline 1px solid var(--border) is the primary separator.Borders do the work shadows would otherwise do. Shadows are subtle, neutral and low.
Colour must never carry meaning alone.Shape, icon and text label accompany every status. From the requirements spec, and it is also the accessibility floor.
Red is reserved.Destructive, failed, blocked, and the "needs a human" signal. It is the only hue in the chrome, which is what makes it legible.
Third-party marks render monochrome.var(--neutral-400), fill:currentColor. A provider band in nine brand colours would be the loudest thing on the page.
No gradients in chrome.Gradients belong to imagery and to the loading sweep. Anywhere else they date the interface.
Dark mode is the same tokens inverted under .dark.No second set of decisions, no second asset. Every surface must re-theme from tokens alone.

RuleWhy
Headings are semibold with negative tracking.−0.02em at UI sizes, tightening to −0.05em on marketing display sizes. Geist at large sizes set loose looks unresolved.
13–14px is the workhorse. Never below 12px.The product is dense and read for hours.
Prose measure is 62–68ch.--width-text: 780px, --width-prose: 68ch. Tool surfaces are dense; reading surfaces are not.
Mono is for identifiers, paths, prices, versions, timestamps and the eyebrow.Anything the reader may need to type back, plus the one label form. Not for body copy, not for emphasis.
Headings balance, body copy wraps pretty.text-wrap: balance on headings, pretty on paragraphs. A two-word orphan under a display heading is the most visible typographic failure the site has.
Inherited rule, overruled in practice

The token readme says "Geist Mono for code, IDs, paths and metadata only — never for labels (that mono-label habit is gone in this theme)." Every Arke surface uses a mono uppercase eyebrow, and it is now a recognisable part of the identity. The eyebrow stands. The rest of the rule holds: mono is not used for ordinary field labels, button text or emphasis.

Everything sits on the 4px grid.A 15px gap is a mistake, not a decision.

09Motion

Motion is choreographed but restrained, and every piece of it must be legible stopped — prefers-reduced-motion flattens animation to 0.01ms globally, so a loop whose meaning lives in the movement becomes a dot or a half-drawn letter.

RuleWhy
Every loop is legible stopped.Reduced motion is a global flatten in this app. A loop must declare its resting state, not inherit a half-finished frame.
Nothing spins.A spinner says "software is busy". Arke's loader says "Arke is starting" — it is the mark, not a spinner with the mark parked inside.
Whole beats at 1.4s–2.4s.Faster reads as anxious.
No spinner without a subject.A loader that cannot say what it is waiting for is a confession.
A number beats an animation.Where real progress is knowable — first-run setup — show the percentage. The loader is for waits with no number.
Hover reveals with opacity, never display.Revealing an action must shift nothing on the page.

10Imagery

The chrome has no colour, so the pictures carry all of it — and they carry the same colour every time. Muted teal-slate, one warm amber accent, painterly cinematic, soft volumetric fog. One accent, deliberately: it is what makes the whole asset set read as one world.

Teal-slate value range, and the single amber accent. Indicative, not a token set — imagery is generated, not styled.

RuleWhy
Every prompt ends with the house style suffix.…painterly cinematic concept art, muted palette with one warm accent, soft volumetric fog, filmic lighting, high detail, no text, no watermark, no border
Character portraits add the cast clause.…consistent character-sheet style, three-quarter view, plain dark neutral backdrop, chest-up framing — so the roster reads as one cast, not six commissions.
One warm accent. Count them.The banner specifies exactly three amber points in the whole frame. Where a generation comes back broadly warm-lit, halve the ambient and keep the lamps as isolated points — a blue/orange split is off-brand even when it is beautiful.
Reuse one portrait everywhere a character appears.That is the product's pitch: one canon, many surfaces. Generating a second portrait for a second screen contradicts the thing being sold.
No text baked into imagery — except the channel banner and avatar.The page supplies all words. The two exceptions are documented in channel-identity.md and exist because YouTube crops kill an overlay.
Product demonstrations use the real interface.Never ask an image or video model to generate UI text. A fabricated screenshot is a lie about the product.
Every loop needs a poster that makes the same claim.Checked with sound off and at prefers-reduced-motion. If the still doesn't say it, the video is decoration.

11The accessibility floor

Not a section that gets traded away. These are already implemented across the site and the requirements spec; the guide records them so they are not lost in the next rewrite.

  • Colour is never the only signal. Shape, icon and text label accompany every status.
  • A visible focus ring on everything focusable — --shadow-focus, 3px of --ring at 50%.
  • prefers-reduced-motion is handled, not just declared. Video is hidden and the poster shown; animated states are replaced with a literal label, not frozen mid-slide.
  • Never below 12px, and default UI text at 13–14px.
  • Every graph and canvas action is reachable by keyboard, with a hierarchical list or script view as an alternative to the visual form.
  • Status palettes account for colour-vision differences, which follows from the first rule but is worth stating separately for the person choosing a new status colour.

12Known exceptions and inherited debt

Stated rather than hidden, so nobody discovers them and assumes they are mistakes.

ExceptionStanding
The token folder is named and branded SpecOne Design System, and the component namespace is SpecOneDesignSystem_b87656.Inherited, accepted for now. Arke adopted the shadcn/ui neutral theme wholesale and re-skinned nothing at token level, which is exactly what an unbranded monochrome system is for. The names are cosmetic debt, not a design problem. Rename when something else forces the folder to move.
The readme in that folder carries SpecOne's writing rules, including "name the user by role, never you" and "never mono for labels".Superseded by this page. That readme describes a different product's voice. Where it conflicts with sections 02–04 here, this page wins.
Jost is loaded in Arke Studio.dc.html for the launch wordmark — 200 58px at .55em tracking.Single documented exception to the one-family rule, and confined to the launch screen. Do not extend it to any other surface.
The marketing site uses weight 550 and a 1200px container, neither of which is in the token set.Deliberate. The site is media-led and display-scale. App surfaces and documents stay on the tokens.
macos-window.jsx and image-slot.js carry @ds-adherence-ignore and use raw hexes and pixels.Vendored starters. They are overwritten on re-copy, so edits do not survive. Not a style violation and not worth fixing.
waitlist.html inlines the colour tokens instead of linking them.Correct. It is a standalone public page. The file already notes: "Every value is copied from the design system, not invented." Any change to a token has to be mirrored there by hand.

13What is not in this page

Component anatomy. Buttons, inputs, cards, badges, status dots and the domain pieces live in _ds/…/components/core/ and in the master document's screens. This page governs how they are worded and coloured, not how they are built.

The Binding decisions. Roughly ninety of them, in Arke Studio.dc.html, each attached to the screen it governs. Section 05 records the form; the document remains the record of the content. Consolidating them into one navigable list is a separate job and a worthwhile one.

Product terminology. The approved storytelling vocabulary — World, Production, Act, Chapter, Sequence, Scene, Beat, Choice, Outcome, Canon, Playthrough — and the four scopes of truth are specified in uploads/arke_studio_narrative_graph_requirements_v2.md §2.1 and §8. They are product rules, not style rules, and they change when the product does.

Image prompts. image-prompts.md and channel-identity.md hold the full prompt set, the delivery contracts and their own decision logs. Section 10 records only the rules that govern all of them.
