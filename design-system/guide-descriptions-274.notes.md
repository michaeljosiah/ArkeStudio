# Turn 274 · A description for readers, the rest after the story

Drawn 8 November 2026 and approved as drawn by the owner the same day; not built yet. It amends turn 234's Guide. Its other half is
Arke Player's turn P29 (in the ArkePlayer repository's design master). To be specified as a
revision of SPEC-059.

## Asked

The owner, looking at a character in Arke Player: the page shows what the author used to make the
story, not what a reader wants to know. "It feels like we need a character description, something
that we want readers to read, and that one is lighter, and then we'll have sections. And the
character description will be available to all readers, regardless of how much they've read the
story. And at the end of the story, we unlock all the sections."

## Why

- Na Love or Juju's Guide opens each character's Appearance from the start and holds their Essence
  until the last chapter. The Appearances were written for the picture model ("dark brown skin kept
  to a high gloss, so that light finds her at the collarbone"); the Essences are the author's notes
  on the whole arc and give the ending away. A reader gets a picture brief and a lock.
- A sheet is a workshop (SPEC-059 §2.1). Choosing which of its sections ship cannot make one of
  them a reader's introduction: nothing in it was written for a reader meeting the character.
- Section-by-section chapters were the answer to "hide or spoil" (SPEC-059 §2.2). The owner prefers
  one moment: everything after the story, nothing before but the description.

## The sample

Na Love or Juju's Guide as the world held it on 8 November 2026: fourteen characters and three
places public, six teasers, no voice line, no settled lore. Who shows after which chapter is the
world's own. Ife's, Ade's and Ade's House's descriptions are drafts for the owner to change. Ife's
two private sections are cut short in 274b and 274c, so this public drawing gives none of the story
away.

## Calls, approved with Player P29 (calls 55–58)

- **One story per world.** Every section waits for one production the author names, the first by
  default. The alternative is each character's own last production, for a world of several stories.
- **Reveal stays in the Player** (Player P29b), so a reader can open the rest before the end.
- **Places follow characters**; lore keeps its own chapter.
- **Roles show to everyone.** Several of Juju's give the story away (Tunde, The friend who was right;
  Segun Adekoya, The man before Ade); they are the sheet's to change.
