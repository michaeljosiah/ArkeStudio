VOICE FIRST

A child who cannot type can still tell the story.

They talk, the studio talks back, and it reads its own questions aloud. Nobody has to spell anything. A parent can take over with a sentence at any point, and every word either of you says is kept.

No reading or typing needed to make something You see the transcript, and who said what Only the words are kept, never the recording
THE HELPERS

Your child meets six helpers, not six features.

Each one does a single job and shows up by name while it works, so a child knows who’s drawing and who’s singing, and a parent knows exactly what touched the story.

Pockets
Listens, and keeps every idea, even the ones you wander away from.
Quill
Writes it down, using your child’s own words wherever they fit.
Inko
Paints the pictures, and matches anything your child drew by hand.
Trill
Reads it aloud and writes the songs, or teaches your voice to do it.
Barnaby
Binds the real book, wraps it in brown paper, puts it in the post.
Nan
Stops anything that needs a grown-up and holds it for you. Never scolds.
every page lists which helpers touched it, and what came from your family instead
