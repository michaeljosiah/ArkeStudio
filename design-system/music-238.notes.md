# Turn 238 · Music as a production

Drawn on 24 October 2026 and not yet approved, with Player P17 (ArkePlayer, drawn the same day).

## Asked

The owner asked: "If we were to support music as a production (or just Artifact) how would we
show that within a world." They approved the recommendations the same day ("Yes"):

- Music is a production, an Album, a Single or a Soundtrack, never a loose artifact.
- It has its own group on a world's page, with square tiles.
- It plays in the Player's audio player and dock, with a track list.
- A track says where it is heard.
- A character or place can name a theme, and a song can be a teaser.

P17 draws the Player. This turn is the author's half: making music at the door (113, 230), the
music production's page, adding its tracks from Bench, a track's lyrics and where it is heard, a
theme in the Guide (234), a song teaser (236), and Export → Arke Player (233).

## What drove the shape

- **A production, not an artifact.** A Bench take on the world's shelf has no cover, size, edition
  or pin, so the Player could not list it, install it or update it. A Single is the smallest thing
  that can.
- **Its own door.** Audio drama and audiobook stay under Write because their work is writing (230).
  Music's work is the songs, so it gets a fourth card, Listen.
- **Tracks come from Bench.** The Studio already makes music in Bench, with the lyricist for
  words, and keeps takes on the shelf. An album is built from kept takes or imported files;
  nothing new generates.
- **Heard in is read, not typed.** A track's take is already placed wherever the world uses it: a
  chapter's sound, an audio drama's cue, a film's cut. The Studio reads those placements, so the
  links cannot drift from the work.
- **One teaser shape.** A song's card keeps 236's 16:9 teaser card. Its square cover shows whole
  over its own blur, as P16 shows a picture that does not fit its row.

## The fourth picture shape

A music cover is square, 1:1, made at 1024 × 1024. The four shapes are:

| Shape | What it is |
|---|---|
| 2:3 | Story covers |
| 9:16 | Tall moments |
| 16:9 | Wide shots and covers |
| 1:1 | Music covers |

## Samples

The world is The Undersong. Its music is three productions:

- *Songs of the Drowned Quarter*, an album of nine tracks, 34:26.
- *Saltmouth Bells*, a single, 3:42.
- *The Bell Watch — Soundtrack*, a soundtrack for the micro drama.

Two tracks are heard in *The Ledger of Nights*: The Drowned Verse in Chapter 9 and Saltmouth Bells
in Chapter 8. Two are themes: The Drowned Verse is the Chorister's and Tide-caller is Maren Kest's.

The track names, lengths, takes, lyrics and sizes are samples. The covers are the design system's
own near-square pictures, shown square: `scene4-shot15` for the album, `launch-harbour` for the
single and `scene4-shot14b` for the soundtrack.

## Open for the owner

1. Music is a fourth door, Listen, rather than a kind under Write: drawn so.
2. An album is exported whole. Releasing it a track at a time, as 236 releases chapters in parts,
   is left for later: drawn so.
3. Lyrics are plain text with the track. Lyrics timed to the song, as the audiobook's Text is, are
   left for later: drawn so.
4. A character or place has one theme, which follows its gate: drawn so.
5. A music cover is square, 1:1 at 1024 × 1024: drawn so.
6. A song teaser is an excerpt of at most 30 seconds, as a clip is: drawn so.
