Interaction decisions · 9

Approved for implementation. 12 October 2026. The owner approved turns 216–219, including turn 216 revision 2. SPEC-054 records implementation and acceptance requirements. These drawings remain the approved UI reference.

A first-class join. A crossfade belongs to an ordered pair of touching still-image clips on the same picture track. Hover or keyboard focus reveals + at the boundary; touch exposes a tappable join control. An existing transition keeps a visible marker that opens its Inspector. This initial design skips video joins and gaps.

Choose at the cut. Click + to open a compact picker anchored to that join, or a bottom sheet on a phone. Show visual previews of available transition types; initially only Crossfade. Choosing a type inserts it immediately with the visible default duration and selects it for adjustment. Opening, dismissing or browsing the picker changes nothing. The Inspector is for subsequent editing, not a prerequisite for insertion. Escape returns focus to the invoking control.

Library drag uses the same action. A Transitions collection supplies the same transition types and defaults. Dragging highlights eligible touching still boundaries. Hovering a target shows a ghost at the fixed cut; dropping inserts and selects the transition in one undoable action. Cancel, invalid targets and empty timeline gaps change nothing. Provide picker access as the equivalent keyboard and touch path. Later transition types must use these same entry points and placement checks.

Replacement is explicit. Dropping onto an occupied join or choosing a replacement shows the existing and proposed type/duration before Replace. Keep existing, Escape and dismissal do not write. Direct manual replacement retains protection rules and explicitly names any protected value it will update. In Suggested, protected settings stay fixed unless the separate explicit release flow authorizes otherwise. An identical replacement is a no-op with no Undo entry.

Fixed timing. The blend straddles the original cut, with half its duration on each side. Each still can supply picture through the blend without trimming, shifting or shortening either clip. The soundtrack clock, cues and total duration stay fixed. No audio source or beat binding is required.

Duration and preview. One second is the initial value. Drag either edge symmetrically, use keyboard nudges or type a frame-snapped duration. The Inspector names the exact cut and blend start/end. Preview transition is an explicit transport action that loops the join with context; comparison and Inspector navigation alone never move the clock.

Bounds. The neighbouring blend windows must not overlap into a three-picture blend or extend beyond available authored coverage. Show a local duration ceiling. Reject an invalid typed value with the reason; no silent retiming, neighbour resizing or shortening. The specification will settle exact frame rounding.

Rendering and removal. Blend the two rendered stills, including their existing visual treatment, then composite continuous composition effects above the result. Removal restores the original hard cut in one Undo step. Preview and export must show the same transition; no generated replacement video is required.

Entry and scope. Apply across images is available from the timeline and transition controls. Choose All still images, Selected images or a typed time range. Keep existing by default. Keep existing leaves existing transitions unchanged. Replace unprotected is explicit; protected presence/type or duration prevents incompatible replacement. Interaction decisions · 6

Two independent rules. Protect transition at this join preserves the presence and Crossfade type for this exact clip pair. Protect duration preserves its current duration. Neither is a generic lock on the whole timeline, and one does not imply the other.

Manual edits stay possible. Direct human edits may change a protected transition and update its protected baseline. A deliberate removal of the transition also removes its own protection in the same Undo transaction. Batch authoring keeps protections unless a separate explicit direct action changes them.

Explicit release. Release names the rule and affected join. Current release is an ordinary undoable edit. A release in review stays pending, retains unrelated rules, and needs explicit Review before Apply. Cancel or discard does not change Current protection. AI text cannot release or claim human approval.

Pair identity. Moving both clips together may move their still-valid join; revalidate duration bounds. Reordering, replacing, removing or separating either clip must not reassign a transition or protection to a different neighbour. Show inactive/broken joins and a direct recovery/removal decision; pending proposals become stale.

Sticky adjustment is separate. A manual pending duration adjustment is retained through revisions of that proposal family, but is not permanent protection. Explicit permission to revise that adjustment is scoped to it. Applying converts the result into ordinary authored transitions; future independent requests obey permanent protection and the new request.

Boundary of this design. This initial capability has one visual transition type. A future type selector must obey the protected-join rule. Video transitions, curve editors, beat snapping and sound crossfades require separate design decisions.

Interaction decisions · 8

Human and Arke parity. Arke may propose creation, duration, removal, batch scope and protection requests using the same join vocabulary as human authoring. A selected join is conversational context. Natural-language requests can resolve scope from current pictures and conversation; ask only for ambiguity, not a mandatory preparatory form.

Composite card in Arke. Show the resolved track/clip-pair scope, version, counts, per-join before/after durations, skipped joins and reasons, shortening choices, protection, manual adjustments and source readiness. Broader effects edits need explicit scope and their own existing acceptance rules. No permanent Changes tab or parallel suggestions inbox.

Explicit preview entry. Engagement, preparation and incoming versions leave Current, transport, loop and panel selection alone. Only a human Review action activates that exact Suggested version. Current/Suggested compare at one clock; Next transition or Preview join moves the clock only when explicitly invoked.

Pending adjustments. Normal Inspector/handle edits change the pending version and display Adjusted by you. Keep current transition or Keep hard cut excludes a proposed change and recomputes the whole result. Retain these decisions across revisions, update and resume; do not reinterpret a broad request as permission to overwrite them.

Arriving revisions. A new version is another composite card with Review version N. It never replaces the active preview automatically. Compatible adjusted durations and protection remain; an impossible preservation is a named conflict with the retained result as default.

Staleness and application. Revalidate the exact pair identities, adjacency, timing, retained neighbouring blends, assets and protection. Changed dependencies disable Apply and require an updated proposal plus explicit review. Apply commits exactly the reviewed coherent result and authorized releases in one Undo entry.

History and resume. Closing review retains its pending state. Discard resolves it without touching Current. Applied/discarded cards are history, not stale pending requests. A later request starts from authored Current, without inheriting old review grants or release authority.

Prototype limits. The companion is an isolated fixture using two public design images, not an application feature or live Arke call. It demonstrates the review boundary and fixed timing; real source resolution, media decoding, export and acoustic playback belong to later specification and implementation.
