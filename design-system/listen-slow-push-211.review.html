Proposed behavior for approval

1 · Proposed, awaiting approval. Turn 211 is design only. It changes no application player, world, narration or generation. The owner must approve this design before implementation. It adds Slow push to the current shared Listen player; all other controls and routes are retained.

2 · One obvious control. Slow push is a labelled switch with On / Off, in the top controls on desktop and beneath the title below 600px. Its new hit area is at least 44px high. It wakes and rests with the existing player chrome; focus or keyboard interaction keeps it discoverable. No additional settings sheet is introduced.

3 · Default and memory. With no saved choice and no system Reduced motion, Slow push starts On. An explicit Off remains Off on reopening. Save only the listener’s choice beside their existing local book/player preferences, not in the world or export manifest. Blocked storage keeps the choice for the open player and does not block playback. A new exported player has its own device/browser choice, not the author’s choice.

4 · Reduced motion wins. While the OS requests Reduced motion, effective Slow push is Off, the switch is disabled and says Off for Reduced motion. Preserve the saved preference without overwriting it. A change to Reduced motion immediately removes the transform without an animated reset. A change back does not restart motion in an already open player: enable the Off switch and let the listener deliberately turn it On. At the next open, resolve the saved choice and current OS setting again.

5 · Gentle movement, same picture area. Use the existing video Slow push amplitude: from 1.00× to 1.06× around the saved focus, centre when absent. Keep the existing contained image rectangle and its letterbox area. Zoom crops within that rectangle; it does not convert Listen to cover-fill or change the phone’s 62% picture region. This explicitly narrows R-69’s never-cropped rule only while the optional effect is On. Off shows the full fitted still. The cover, title/poster and missing-media fallback remain static.

6 · Audio is the clock. Progress is the clamp of (chapter audio time − picture start) / picture hold, through the next picture or chapter end. Pause freezes it, buffering cannot drift, seeking and speed changes immediately select the corresponding position. Turning On mid-picture uses that same position. Resuming never restarts the push. The existing picture crossfade remains separate; each side uses its own clock and crop.

7 · Clips stay as chosen. Slow push never transforms an attached AI motion clip or its fallback still; Repeat / Hold, muting, focus and current reduced-motion clip behavior stay intact. The preference can remain On ready for the next still. It never starts generation, uses a provider, downloads a model, rewrites media or adds cost.

8 · One player, two homes. Implement in the shared framework-free audiobook player so Listen and newly exported HTML player packages match. Carry existing picture hold and focus through its typed input. No new world field or schema boundary is needed. Existing MP4 export Slow push remains independent and unchanged.

9 · Acceptance after approval. Verify saved Off, missing/denied storage, live OS preference changes in both directions, pause/resume/buffering, seeks, playback speed, picture changes, no picture/cover, clip and unavailable clip, chapter scope and whole book, long chapter titles, 320px and 200% text, keyboard switch use and the actual packaged player. Use matched-state screenshots against these HTML frames; do not rewrite approved drawings to hide a mismatch.
