Proposed rule 1. Approval first. This turn is proposed, not approved. No UI or runtime implementation follows until the owner approves this turn. Motion and captions are two independent slices; approval of drawings does not establish that accurate alignment exists.

Proposed rule 2. Animate starts from the chosen still. Add Animate only to a block that has a usable still. The source image is the start frame, not an identity reference. Only currently available models with a first-frame route are offered; use the shared capability query, never a hard-coded list. The prompt is a small editable motion brief, drafted from the shot, keeping pose, objects and camera unless the author changes it.

Proposed rule 3. Price and time before generation. Model, supported length, delivered resolution, estimated price and machine/provider time stand above one Generate action. Local means no API charge, not no time. No hidden batch, automatic retry, model download, upscaling or follow-up purchase. A materially changed quote requires a fresh confirmation. A source below export size is explicitly scaled; upscaling remains a separate operation in the existing Bench.

Proposed rule 4. A candidate until chosen. Generation leaves the currently selected visual in use. Review offers a muted clip, actual measured dimensions and length, and a preview with the reading including the repeat boundary. Use clip alone attaches it. Keep still leaves the candidate in the Library. A completion for an old block, source revision or changed selection is filed for review and never displaces current work.

Proposed rule 5. Preserve the still. Keep the original still, look, shot and focus as the picture source. Store the chosen clip as optional motion attached to that source, with file/artifact identity, measured duration, source digest and end behavior; do not overload the existing still file without distinguishing media kind. Old still-only records keep their exact meaning. The first motion write needs the world-version safeguard required by the strict older record schema.

Proposed rule 6. Repeat or hold, never audible. Repeat is the approved default, over the existing picture hold until the next picture or chapter end, not merely the source block length. Hold last frame plays once, then holds its final frame. A shorter hold truncates either. Clips are always muted; the chapter mix is the only sound. Slow push only affects stills. Player, packaged player, preview and MP4 share the visual clock, seek result, crop and one-second crossfade. Reduced-motion playback shows the retained still; exporting a chosen clip remains explicit.

Proposed rule 7. Source and changes remain recoverable. Use still reverses the attachment without deleting the source or the filed clip. A join/split/reset keeps 198’s first-picture ownership and hidden picture records, including their motion; no clip is moved to another source by guess. Missing or changed media is named. Playback can show the retained still with a notice; export must ask for an explicit still choice or refuse, never silently export a different visual.

Proposed rule 8. One caption choice. Style is Phrases or Highlight current word, shown only for Burned in or Both. Phrases remains the default. Retain the existing position, size, two-line phrase breaks and safe margins. Highlight one current word in yellow at its measured interval, then return it to white; do not accumulate yellow or sweep letters. Between words and through silence no word is highlighted. Plain MP4 text/SRT/VTT sidecars remain plain. The preview uses exactly the same cue and timing plan as the render.

Proposed rule 9. Accurate timing is a prerequisite. Current timedWords is character-proportional interpolation. The grouped reader times pauses and interpolates words inside speech stretches, then discards the word sequence. Neither is evidence for accurate word highlighting. The caption slice requires an evaluated acoustic word aligner or provider word timings mapped to the authored text, with confidence and unmatched-word reporting. There is no fabricated percentage, no implicit use of approximate times and no claim that this is rendering only.

Proposed rule 10. Prepare saved audio, not another read. Once a supported aligner exists, selecting Highlight current word reveals Prepare word timing for missing or stale blocks. Preparation is a cancellable local operation over the saved current reading, with progress and measured/unknown time, never new narration. Bind timings to audio content/artifact, authored text and alignment version, then transform them through trims, offsets and the chapter mix. One reading edits only its affected timing. On failure the author can use phrases; mixed silent fallback is not allowed.

Proposed rule 11. Review and invalidate. Review timing names stale or uncertain blocks and lets the author play the line with its word highlight. Retry preparation is explicit. Render with highlighting waits until all selected narration blocks have complete accepted timing; accepting a split boundary or name transcription never certifies individual word timing. Overlapping voices and reactions need a defined caption lane before their blocks qualify. A source/mix/text change invalidates the affected plan and export cache. Do not provide a blanket Keep all for uncertain words.

Proposed rule 12. Responsive and bounded. The Picture entry remains a single action. Animate and review use one active modal, with keyboard focus trapped, Escape/Close returning to Picture and background presses inert. A 390 px sheet has one column, reachable 44 px actions and independently scrolling content above its persistent footer; the quote stays adjacent to Generate. Desktop is the same order. No extra chapter toolbar actions, motion timeline, caption colour picker or new generation dashboard in this slice.
