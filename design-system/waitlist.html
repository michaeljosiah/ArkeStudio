Three mechanics · one studio

Built to remember what other tools forget.

Most creative tools are organised around projects. Arke starts one level higher — with the world every project shares.

01

Nothing changes until you accept.

Every draft arrives as a proposal. Arke works out what it would affect, shows you the ripple, and then waits. The screen is the gate.

02

Canon that knows when to refuse.

Ask the world anything. Every claim cites the entry behind it — and when the record is silent, Arke says so instead of inventing behind your back.

03

Identity that travels.

A main photo and one character sheet become the reference set, and they follow the character into every frame, on every provider that takes them.
