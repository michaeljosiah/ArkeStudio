Interaction decisions

New preset, shared effect model. Air Particles joins the existing procedural Effects library. It uses the established independent visual target, exact interval, optional audio binding, fades and curves. It does not replace Floating dust or Floating lights or reinterpret existing projects.

Visible character. A fuller mixture of fine specks, textured discs and soft foreground particles. Randomness is smooth and repeatable, with dispersed initial coverage and airflow that avoids persistent clumps. The updated motion study is visual evidence, not a production renderer or particle budget.

Appearance and entry. Keep basic Amount, Size, Opacity and Colour with Texture, Softness and Depth variation. Existing emitting area, direction, fades and curves remain available through the effect inspector; the focused drawings show new controls. Cancel placement writes nothing; Add is one undoable edit.

Pattern and continuity. Store pattern identity and a versioned motion profile. New pattern deliberately regenerates the arrangement in one undoable edit; reseeking never rerolls it. Trimming and splitting retain original phase; audio reaction does not reroll the pattern. A protected pattern constrains Arke but permits a direct human edit with a new protected baseline.

Target coordinates. Composition effects see the assembled picture; scoped effects stay within their target’s visible picture/matte. The emitting area sets where particles begin, not a new collision wall or arbitrary scene mask. Cuts and invisible target passages do not restart the simulation.

Approval boundary. These are proposed designs, not an accepted specification. Renderer limits, determinism across hosts, caching and exact compositing order need specification and measured preview/export acceptance before implementation.

Organic distributionIndependent variation and non-converging airflow avoid persistent clumps. Gentle contacts occur within similar depth layers.
Interaction decisions

Motion controls. Start with Speed, Airflow variation, Gentle particle deflections and Picture boundary: Open flow or Soft bounce. Keep solver internals out of the normal editor. The proposal uses Soft bounce and gentle deflections as this preset’s initial defaults, subject to owner review.

What physics means. Particles retain inertia and follow smooth varied airflow, with gentle contacts among similar depth layers. Depth is a visual approximation, not reconstructed 3D. Soft bounce uses target picture bounds; it does not detect people, furniture or walls inside footage.

Randomness without rerolling. Changing airflow changes the motion; New pattern changes its authored seed. The same settings and timestamp must reproduce the same result. No hidden reseeding on play, render, audio silence, proposal arrival or seek.

Audio independence. Reaction Off requires no analysis. On explicitly chooses an audible placed source. Silence, mute and outside-source time keep baseline atmosphere. Existing simple presets and Advanced beat/bass/energy controls apply; initial response properties remain brightness, emission, size or speed. Audio does not toggle contacts/boundaries or reseed.

Performance and fallback. Preview may lower raster resolution under the existing quality control, never silently reduce population, disable contacts or change the simulation. A source/profile that cannot render must show a reason. Performance limits and checkpointing are specification questions.

Interaction decisions

First blend modes. Propose Normal, Screen and Add, with independent Opacity, for procedural effect instances and individual visual media clips. Existing content defaults to Normal and retains its old opacity. Air Particles may choose Screen as a new preset default. No track-level or arbitrary group blend modes in this first scope.

Stack semantics. A clip blends against the content beneath it at its existing picture-stack position. A scoped effect blends into its target before that target joins the composition; a composition effect follows assembled picture and precedes captions. Explicit ordering never moves time, changes soundtrack or replaces crossfade settings. Crossfades and scoped effects need exact render-order specification.

Transparency and source formats. Generated particles have alpha between particles; Normal must not add a black plate. A black-backed imported video remains opaque source media, with Screen/Add doing compositing. Preserve source colour and alpha through supported proxies. ProRes 422 HQ has no alpha; an alpha-capable source needs an alpha-preserving path. EXR, camera RAW and plugins are outside this design.

Recorded overlay timing. Imported overlays keep ordinary clip placement, trim and source duration. Blend changes never extend a 7.92-second source across a song. Loop/repeat authoring needs a separate design decision; no silent freeze, stretch or repetition.

Granular protection. Extend existing property protection to Blend mode, Opacity, Pattern, Texture, Softness, Depth variation and each new physics setting. Whole-effect or fixed-passage properties, exact Start/End boundaries and fixed-passage coverage remain different rules. Clip blend/opacity use the same visible property rule vocabulary on that clip, without manufacturing an effect.

Human edits and release. Direct human edits stay available and update a protected baseline while retaining its rule. Explicit Release names property, object, value and scope. Current release is undoable. Pending release prepares a fresh card marked Release on Apply, retains the active preview, and requires another explicit Review. Arke cannot grant or infer release.

Preset or structural changes. Switching preset, removing, replacing or splitting cannot evade a protected blend, seed or property. Show incompatible preserved settings and ask for a named decision; never reset them through a preset default. Compatible segmentation retains phase and fixed passage rules.

Interaction decisions

Shared authoring. Arke can propose all supported preset, appearance, motion, blend, opacity, placement and optional audio operations using the same validated commands as human editing. Existing target/time/source bindings remain for an appearance-only request. A request for fuller particles does not edit picture, sound or transitions.

Natural requests, explicit result. Requests may start in ordinary chat or from the selected effect. “Across the video” resolves the current picture delivery range; “whole song” names a chosen placed audio interval. The card shows the resolved scope before review. No mandatory music track or hidden prerequisite selection.

Composite card and explicit entry. Native Arke cards show named objects, before/after properties, scope, exclusions, preserved rules, manual adjustments and release decisions. Preparing, receiving and resuming an unreviewed card leave Current active. Review in timeline selects one exact version. There is no permanent Changes tab.

Sticky manual review. Normal controls edit Suggested. Keep current and Exclude revise the coherent candidate. Their decisions and manual property/curve edits persist through revisions, updates and resume within the proposal family. “Make this less busy” does not authorize overwriting them. Explicit permission to revise an adjustment is scoped and does not release protection.

Incoming and stale versions. An incoming version never replaces the displayed version until Review. Stale base/target/source/protection disables Apply; Update produces another card for explicit review. A changed release baseline needs a new named Release action. Closing retains pending work; Discard cancels it and pending releases.

One reviewed transaction. Apply commits precisely the reviewed arrangement and human-authorized releases in one revision and Undo entry. Comparisons use one playhead and soundtrack. Temporary audition visibility is not an authored setting. Later independent requests start from Current and inherit no old release or review grants.

Decisions for this review

Proposed defaults: dispersed Air Particles, gentle contacts, Soft bounce and Screen. Initial modes: Normal / Screen / Add on effects and visual clips. No scene-wall detection, automatic overlay looping or plugin support.

After design approval, specify blend colour/alpha rules and render order, supported source proxies, deterministic physics and population limits, property protection across passages, and preview/export acceptance. SPEC-053 supplies the existing effects and review baseline; these additions are not yet part of its accepted scope.
