# Turn 275 · The Cut's Library, another production's shots

Drawn 11 November 2026 and approved as drawn by the owner the same day; not built yet. The design
turn issue 2953 (epic 2952) asks for before any code.

## Asked

Issue 2953: a production's Cut should be able to use a shot made in another production of the same
world. Draw the Library with another production chosen: where the other productions sit in the
existing select, a row for one of that production's shots, the row after it has been added, and the
empty state. Use the current Library frame as the base. No new panel, sheet or screen.

## Why

- A world holds several productions and their takes do not cross. A trailer cut from a film's
  finished shots, or a film that reuses an establishing shot, means exporting and importing by hand.
- The Library already browses another world's media and copies it in. Another production of the
  same world is the nearer case and fits the same select, list, picked-row action and `from` line.
- A copy, not a link: the source production is never written, and a later take there cannot change
  a cut here.

## What is drawn

## The sample

Harbour lights, the film with no story of its own that turn 220's Cut shell draws, borrowing from
Saltlight in the same world. The shell is 220's own script, unchanged outside the Library column.
The Library is drawn as it ships for such a production: the chips All, Not in the cut and Audio,
then the select, then All kinds. Saltlight's scene 4 has no accepted take for shot 3, so the list
goes from Shot 2 to Shot 4.

## Calls, approved as drawn

- **This production.** The select's first option reads `This world` today. With a group named
  `This world's productions` under it that says the wrong thing, so it is drawn as `This production`.
- **No mark for a shot already added.** Adding the same shot twice makes two files. A mark would
  need the copy to remember which take it came from; left out until it is missed.
- **The file's name.** `Saltlight · SC 4 · Shot 4`, so it reads on its own in the Library and on a
  lane; the `from` line repeats the production as it does for another world.
